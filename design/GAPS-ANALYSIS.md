# Where the encyclopedia is thin

*Regenerate with `cd site && node analyse.mjs`. Figures below are from the run at 201 entries.*

## Method

Six numbers per entry. **Words**. **Inbound**, how many entries point at it. **Outbound**.
**Cited**, whether a scene in the manuscript exists. **Gap markers**, bracketed absences.
And **hedges**: how often the compilers say some version of *we have not been able to establish*.

Two of those find different problems.

**Inbound against words** finds structural holes. An entry the volume leans on constantly and
says little about reads fine alone and only fails once forty others have promised it.

**Hedges** find undecided worldbuilding. Where the archive admits it does not know, that is
usually not voice. It is the compiler hitting a hole in the notes and papering it.

# YOURS TO DECIDE

Nine entries hedge two or more times. That is the archive admitting, in its own voice, that
the worldbuilding underneath is not settled.

1. **The Council of Riflo.** 14 inbound, hedges twice. The entry cannot say how a seat is
   filled or where the Council's authority comes from. This is the government of the most
   distinctive people in the book. **Highest-value single decision on this page.**
2. **Maspin family structure.** Three hedges, the most in the corpus. They call themselves one
   family and appear to mean it literally, and the entry admits we got no further.
3. **Maspin naming taboos.** Councillors unnamed, ships unnamed, personal names shortened.
   Three whats, no why.
4. **The Nhath homeworld.** Entirely inferred from the language, because there is no survey and
   no name. Conspicuous next to a fully built grammar.
5. **House Kafkee.** Cannot say whether the Flamebearer office carries a duty.
6. **The Kitalia platform collapse.** Who the cells were is withheld on purpose. Who
   investigated is blank by accident.
7. **Cillus.** His whereabouts are a deliberate gap. Why he left is an accidental one.
