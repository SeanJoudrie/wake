## Naming stock still unused
Directions the corpus has no examples of, held for future worlds and peoples.

- **Yoruba / Bantu / Amharic** — the one real absence. gb clusters, Adé- and
  Olú- prefixes, -tunde and -wale endings; m- and ng' openings, -wa; Tesfaye,
  Mekonnen, Bekele. Assign to worlds that were never schooled by somebody else.
- **Basque for Katanzaria** — tx, tz, x, -aga, -arri. A language isolate for a
  people who claim to be related to nobody. Complication: Calsco Fritt is
  already Katanzarian and does not read this way.
- **Welsh for Monver** — Ll-, -wyn, dd, Bryn, Rhys, Emrys, Cadoc. Mountain
  people who fought their own empire and kept their government. Brynor Kolt is
  already halfway there.
- **Icelandic patronymics for Marfeld** — -son, -dottir. A world that has kept
  a muster book by name for eleven centuries should have a naming system that
  is itself a record.
- **Polynesian for Kitalia** — vowel-heavy, glottal stops, -nui, Kai-. A world
  with no land.
- **Finnish for a cold isolate** — doubled vowels, -nen, -kka. Reads unrelated
  to everything around it.
- **Nahuatl for a non-human people** — -tl, tz, x. Alien to an English ear
  without being invented.
