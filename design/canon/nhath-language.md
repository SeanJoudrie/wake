# Nhath — language reference

Source document, supplied by the author. Canon. Do not extend without a ruling.

## Aesthetic
Dry harshness, like desert wind through rock. Slow Italian rhythm, deliberate and
measured. Readable first, foreign through the vowels. Diphthongs (vai, rei, kou,
gai, vei), umlauts (a, o, u with diaeresis), simple clusters (sc, ch, ts, kr, tr),
guttural g / k / r. No consonant soup. Delivery is quiet intensity: consonants
bite, vowels flow. Calculated predator, not angry beast.

## Grammar
Spanish-style conjugation, endings change by subject. Turkish and Japanese
influence: systematic but not rigid. Word order flexible.
- `-ing` = o + verb (oka = coming, ogrehor = sleeping)
- past perfect = a + verb (atrei = had killed, asprato = had searched)

## Numbers, base eight
1-8 ka da ga ra ma ba va sa
Block prefixes, in order: Ko Do Go Ro Mo Bo Va Sa Ke De Ge Re Me Be Ve Se,
then compounded (Kabeoka, Dabeoda, Gabeoga ...). 87 = Deoga. 88 = Deosa.

## Names
[Physical trait]-[body part] [spiritual element]-[family role] [lineage number]
Kakavai-Shtuv Aroniela-Kipo Deoga = Strongskull Moonchild 87
Surname passes unchanged. Number increments each generation. Trait is the child's,
not the parent's. New clans start at 1.
Formal short form fuses the given name (Kashtuv). Intimate clips again (Kash).

## Culture, as the language reveals it
No neutral word for an outsider: krayd covers they, other species and enemy.
A dedicated word for prey. No word for please, but a word for thank you: they do
not ask favours, they command or trade. Base eight from four fingers per hand.
Sun is short and harsh, moon is long and flowing, and the moon is the sacred one.
