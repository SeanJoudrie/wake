Method

Six numbers per entry. Words. Inbound, how many entries point at it. Outbound. Cited, whether a scene in the manuscript exists. Gap markers, bracketed absences. And hedges: how often the compilers say some version of we have not been able to establish.

Two of those find different problems.

Inbound against words finds structural holes. An entry the volume leans on constantly and says little about reads fine alone and only fails once forty others have promised it.

Hedges find undecided worldbuilding. Where the archive admits it does not know, that is usually not voice. It is the compiler hitting a hole in the notes and papering it.
