# APPRENTICESHIP
`INTACT`

You work for nothing and you leave with a trade. That is the whole arrangement, and most of the galaxy runs on it.

**How it is put to you.** Plainly, usually across a bench, by somebody with no time to be delicate about it: *I will teach you. I cannot pay you. You will leave here able to do this.*

And it is not a trick. Most small businesses genuinely cannot pay a second wage. The offer is honest, everybody involved knows exactly what it is, and most people take it, because the alternative is paying somebody else for the same thing.

**You can apprentice to almost anything stable.** Carpenter, electrician, mechanic, cook, medic, librarian, even police, where it means ride-alongs and a great deal of paperwork. The only requirement is finding somebody willing to take you.

**Which is the catch.** Nothing here is decided by a qualification. It is decided by whether a person who does the work is prepared to have you in the room, and that comes down to who your family knows. It is a class system that never had to be written down, because it never had to be enforced.

**Most people go through two or three before anything sticks.** Which produces the characteristic fact about anybody you meet in this galaxy: everybody has a year of something. A year of engines, a year of stonework, a year of setting bones. Half-finished trades, everywhere, in everyone.

It is also how skill survived the war at all. The machines went and the schools mostly went, and the knowledge came through anyway, hand to hand, because that is the only way it was ever really transmitted.

**And it is why the Guard fills its ranks without trying.** Every recruiter in the galaxy is walking into rooms full of young people who have been told, honestly and repeatedly, that learning something costs them four years of unpaid work.

The Guard pays you to learn.
