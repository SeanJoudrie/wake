# THE ASTEROID COLONY DOCKING PAD
`INTACT`

Standard single-berth landing facility fitted to inhabited fragments of the Wake field. One per fragment.

**Specification.** Filed. Surface, dimensions, load rating, approach lighting, and the finish, which is specified as azure across every installation in the schedule.

**Use.** Weekly. The schedule provides for one arrival per fragment per week, delivering supplies and consumables and lifting certified Corefuel. The manifests for the lifts are `INTACT`, tonnage and duty recorded per landing.

The manifests for the deliveries are held in a different series and are incomplete.

**Communications.** The schedule notes an orbital condition interrupting radio contact with the fragments for a period of several days at intervals of approximately six months, and instructs vessels to work to the published timetable during it.
