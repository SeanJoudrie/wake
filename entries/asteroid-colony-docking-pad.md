# THE ASTEROID COLONY DOCKING PAD
`INTACT`

One landing pad per rock, painted azure, and the only door in or out.

A ship comes once a week. It brings food, water, plant consumables, school materials and mail, and it takes away certified Corefuel. That is the whole rhythm of life out there: six days of nothing and one day when the world briefly connects to the rest of the galaxy.

Twice a standard year the orbit puts the fragments somewhere the radio cannot reach for several days. The safety schedule's advice for that period is to work to the published timetable, which is another way of saying wait.
