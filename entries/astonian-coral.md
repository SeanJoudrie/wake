# ASTONIAN CORAL
`INTACT`

Reef, standing in open air, where a hedge should be.

Astonia had an ocean and does not any more, and the coral simply stayed where it was. It stands across the whole equatorial belt, eight metres in places, dry, in pinks and oranges and a blue that keeps glowing after dark.

It does not grow. It does not rot either. There is exactly enough water in the sand to hold it where it is, indefinitely.

**The colours are wrong on purpose.** They look garish to human eyes and they were never meant for human eyes. The fish that lived here saw further up the spectrum than we do, and in that light, what looks lurid to a visitor was camouflage.

**People keep it alive.** There is a spray bottle by the door in most houses in the settled belt, and misting the nearest formation is a daily thing that everybody does. No law requires it. Ask why and you get a different answer from everyone.

**And it protects them.** A lifetime spent breathing over these reefs is what Guard medicine credits for Astonian resistance to eleven diseases the rest of the galaxy has no answer for.
