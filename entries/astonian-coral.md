# ASTONIAN CORAL
`INTACT`

Reef structures standing above ground across the equatorial belt of Astonia, the ocean having been removed.

**Condition.** The organisms neither grow nor die. Substrate moisture is sufficient to sustain them and insufficient to permit growth. The condition has been stable for the whole period of the survey record.

**Colour.** Vivid across the full visible range, and to human observers garish. The pre-war biological literature attributes this to the visual range of the fauna the reefs evolved alongside, which extended beyond the human one, and records that the colouring was, in that light, concealment.

**Maintenance.** The reefs are kept alive by the population. Municipal returns record the issue of misting apparatus at household scale, and the practice of daily hand-misting of local formations is universal in the settled districts. The Restoration records that no ordinance requires it.

**Medical significance.** Lifelong exposure to reef organisms is the factor to which Guard medical survey attributes the Astonian resistance to a range of pathogens for which no vaccine stock exists.
