# THE ASTONIAN DIALECT
`TESTIMONY`

`[NO LIVING SPEAKERS RECORDED]`

The way Astonia used to talk. One person wrote it down, and then it was gone.

**The survey.** 4,100 words, a sketch of the sounds, and eleven pages of stories taken down from speakers. Done by a single investigator using a transcription system of their own invention which they never explained. No recording exists. The investigator's name appears on nothing else, anywhere.

**How it ended.** After the annexation, the schools went to sponsored religious foundations on contract. Residential attendance, and teaching in Galactic Standard. Inside eleven years they had ninety-four per cent of the children.

One generation. That is all it takes.

Our own survey of the world in GC 4457 found no speaker.

**Two attempts to bring it back**, both by Astonian associations, both working from the same notes because there is nothing else, both suspended.
