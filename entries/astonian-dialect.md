# THE ASTONIAN DIALECT
`TESTIMONY`

`[NO LIVING SPEAKERS RECORDED]`

The prior speech of Astonia. Attested in one linguistic survey, uncorroborated, conducted by a single investigator whose notes this archive holds and whose name appears on nothing else.

**The survey.** 4,100 lexical items, a phonological sketch, and eleven pages of transcribed narrative. The transcription conventions are the investigator's own and are not documented. No recording survives.

**Loss.** Education on Astonia was transferred to sponsored religious foundations under contract following the annexation of GC 4419. The contracts specify residential attendance and instruction in Galactic Standard. Enrolment reached 94 per cent of the eligible cohort within eleven years.

The dialect ceased to be transmitted within one generation. The Restoration's survey of the world in GC 4457 recorded no speaker.

**Attempts at recovery.** Two, both by Astonian associations, both from the same survey notes, both suspended.
