# ASTONIAN MILK
`INTACT`

Dairy produced on Astonia from herds maintained on local forage. Exported as liquid and as solids.

**Export returns, GC 4461:** 411,880 tonnes of solids, 88,200 tonnes liquid equivalent. Royalty payable to the Royal houses on the same: 1,204,880 credits.

**Dietary factor.** Guard medical survey attributes the Astonian bone density and muscle mass measurements to a factor present in this milk. The factor is not present in dairy produced elsewhere from the same stock, and is attributed in turn to the local forage.

Attempts to produce the forage off-world are recorded in the agricultural returns of three worlds. All three are recorded as unsuccessful.
