# ASTONIAN MILK
`INTACT`

Dairy from herds that graze on a forage growing nowhere else in the galaxy, and the reason Astonians are built the way they are.

Guard medical survey credits something in this milk for bone density and muscle mass well above the human mean. Take the same animals somewhere else and the effect disappears, because it is the grass, not the cows.

Three worlds have tried to grow that forage off Astonia. All three failed.

Four hundred thousand tonnes of solids go out a year, and the Royal houses take a royalty on every tonne of it under an instrument they signed decades ago.
