# ASTONIANS
`INTACT`

Human population of Astonia. Levied under the annexation instrument of GC 4419.

**Physical survey.** Guard medical survey, GC 4416, three years before annexation, records mean bone density and mean skeletal muscle mass substantially above the human reference range, and attributes both to a dietary factor present in locally produced milk and not reproduced elsewhere. The survey recommends further study of the dietary factor. No further study is filed.

A second survey of the same year records resistance across a range of pathogens for which no vaccine stock exists, and attributes it to lifelong exposure to reef organisms. The same recommendation appears. The same absence follows it.

**Service.** Astonian personnel enter Guard service by levy and not by contract, the levy being a term of the annexation instrument. Yield GC 4461: 41,208. Failure rate at basic training: 3.1 per cent, against a member-world mean of 9.7.

**Language.** The prior dialect is not in use. Catalogued separately.

**Note.** The two medical surveys are dated GC 4416. The annexation instrument is dated GC 4419. The Restoration records the dates and draws nothing from them.
