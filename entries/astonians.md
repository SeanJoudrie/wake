# ASTONIANS
`INTACT`

The people of a world with no sunrise, and the reason the Guard wanted it.

They are stronger than the human mean and they are harder to make ill, and both of those were measured and written down three years before anybody annexed anything.

**Strength.** Bone density and muscle mass well above the range. Guard survey credits the milk, which comes from herds grazing a forage that grows nowhere else. It is not training and it is not selection. They are simply built that way, and they were built that way before the Guard arrived.

**Immunity.** A lifetime breathing over the dry reefs leaves them shrugging off eleven diseases nobody has a vaccine for.

Both surveys recommended further study. Neither study happened. The annexation happened.

**Since.** Astonians do not enlist, they are levied, under a clause of the instrument. Forty-one thousand a year. Their failure rate at basic training is a third of the member-world average, which surprises nobody who has read the first half of this entry.
