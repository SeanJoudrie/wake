# ATMOSPHERIC MASKS
`INTACT`

Portable respiratory apparatus for persons whose physiology is not suited to the local atmosphere. In current manufacture.

Patterns are produced for eleven recorded physiologies. The Vitula pattern is the most widely produced, Vitula labour being employed across a large number of worlds on which Vitula cannot breathe unaided.

Purchase is generally by the employer and is generally recoverable from the worker's wage. The recovery terms appear in the standard labour instruments and are filed.
