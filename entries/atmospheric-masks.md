# ATMOSPHERIC MASKS
`INTACT`

The most common object in the galaxy, because most people are breathing somewhere they did not evolve to breathe.

At the cheap end it is a moulded cup over the mouth and nose with a filter cartridge and a strap, sold everywhere, replaced constantly, and thrown away when the cartridge browns. At the other end it is a machined respirator built into a suit and worn for a working lifetime.

**What it tells you about a person.** Everything. A mask says which world's air a body was built for, and therefore where that body is standing now and why. A crew stepping off a ramp will hold up a small reader, watch it flash from red to green, and pull their masks off with the relief of people who have just been told they get to be comfortable.

Some species wear them permanently offworld and have done for so long that their faces are essentially a matter of hearsay. A Gashi bounty hunter in a good suit is a pair of gold-black eyes above a piece of engineering, and there are people who have known him for years and could not tell you what is under it.

**The etiquette.** You do not ask. Not what is under it, not what the air was like at home, and not why he has never taken it off.
