# THE BATTLE OF RUDCUFF
`TESTIMONY`

One of the Guard's first, fought by the only two battalions it had, and the day it learned what it had signed up for.

**The set-up.** A colony held by Targaths. First and Second Battalion, which at that point was the entire army, every one of them a volunteer. The objective was to take the ground. Nobody expected much difficulty.

**What actually happened.** The Targaths had built a magnetic web through the crust and were waiting. It came on as the fleet made its approach.

The ships in the outer ring went first, dragged out of the sky and into the surface. On the ground the men could not move: armour, weapons, everything pulled flat, pinned in the open with the sky coming down around them. Whether they fought back is genuinely not known.

**The reinforcements found nothing.** Not a defeat, not a rout, not a line that had broken and run. There was simply nobody left to have any of those things happen to.

**Why it is still spoken about.** Because it was survivable for exactly one reason: the second wave was late. And a man who came in with that second wave has spent thirty years carrying both halves of that, the guilt and the gratitude, and now sits in a chair with an old map on the wall behind him.

He calls them real heroes. He also, without noticing he is doing it, gets nostalgic about it, in the specific way of a man remembering the last time his job was simple.
