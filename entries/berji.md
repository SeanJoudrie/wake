# BERJI
`CONTESTED`

Monveri national in Guard service. Prior service in the Monver Planetary Defence Force.

**The contested point.** The Monver Planetary Defence Force register records service, a theatre, and a decoration. The Guard personnel record for the same individual begins at contract and records nothing before it.

Both are accurate on their own terms. The Guard's record begins at contract by rule, absorbed formations entering as individuals and not as units. The effect is that a man who fought a war before he was employed has, in his employer's account, not fought one.

This is not particular to him. It is the condition of every absorbed Planetary Defence Force veteran in the establishment, and the Restoration prints it here because this is the file in which we first noticed it.
