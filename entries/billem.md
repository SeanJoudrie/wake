# CORPORAL BILLEM
`RECOVERED`

The man who was in charge, and the reason somebody else ended up in charge.

**What happened to him.** He was breaching a door on a cartel compound when one of his own people put a fragmentation grenade through a house that should have taken a flashbang. Every gas tank in the building went up. A second later the rounds came through the door he was standing in, and took his leg out from under him.

He killed the man who shot him before he sat down, and reported it in one word that does not appear anywhere else in this archive.

> *"I frosted the fucking Crag that shot me, so it's clear here."*

**How he took it.** Mask off, back against a wall, one hand pressed hard into his own thigh, and furious. Not at the wound. At the man who caused it, who was standing four feet away and is described in the record only as *Private Dumbass*.

> *"Look I'll be fine, but I just cannot keep up with you."*

That sentence is the whole of him. He is bleeding, he is not getting up, and the part he chooses to say out loud is an apology for slowing the assault down.

**What it cost, and what it did.** Six or seven hostiles were still holed up in the compound. He could not go. So a staff sergeant took three men forward and finished it, and the returns for that action read *zero Guard fatalities, the principal captured alive, the compound intact*, followed by the phrase that gets him into this volume at all: **all without a corporal.**

He stayed on the net the entire time, running the evacuation from the floor.

> *"Copy that. EVAC is about an hour out. Regroup at entry."*

**Afterwards.** The ankle is described by a friend as being in about a million pieces, and the same friend reckons he is quietly relieved: he had been going out every other week for a long time, and a stretch in a hospital bed is the first uninterrupted rest anybody has been able to give him.

*[Compiler's note: the grenade is attributed in one place to a private on his own team and in another to a named man on the team that came to help him. Both accounts are held here. Somebody, on the day, decided to cover for somebody.]*
