# BIODIUM
`INTACT`

A combustible fuel extracted on Pashmirské. It is not Corefuel and is not comparable to it.

**Origin.** It is the residue of a rainforest that covered a large part of the world and dried out a very long time before anybody arrived on it. The deposit is finite, is not being added to, and was laid down by an ecosystem that no living record describes.

**What it is for.** Domestic and light industrial use. Heating, cooking, generators, small plant. It does not fly anything and no vessel of any class runs on it.

**What it is worth.** Enough. Pashmirské is not a wealthy world and is not a poor one, and the difference is this deposit. It is the reason the administration can pay for what it provides, and the reason the world has not needed to ask anybody for anything since the war ended.
