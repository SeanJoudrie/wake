# BOTIN TOBIN
*also: Botin*
`TESTIMONY`

A machine that rows boats by hand. He carries passengers for no fare and accepts donations, which he does not ask for.

**Purpose.** Rowing is what he was built to do and it is the whole of what he was built to do. He uses the word *purpose* for it himself. His capacity to choose otherwise is limited, and he does not describe that as a problem.

**Origin.** A city built on water, worked by boats that were rowed by hand. He was a rower there. He cannot name the planet. His processing has degraded far enough that he cannot recover the name and does not try to.

**The water.** On the world he came from, the water did not damage him. On the world he is on now, it does. He rows anyway. The damage is continuous and he is not being repaired.

**The standing questions.** Passengers raise the same three points with him and he gives the same three answers.

Told that he does not have to row if it hurts him: *"I don't care."*

Asked whether it is painful, and whether it is lonely for a machine built for a city to be working a river in jungle country: *"Yeah, whatever."*

Asked whether he wants to find meaning: *"You can find all you want. Mine was built in. I'm meant to row. That's my purpose."*

**Manner.** Cheerful, unhurried and funny. He is easy company on the water.
