# THE BURIAL MOON
`INTACT`

Moon given over entirely to interment of Guard personnel. No civil population. Caretaker establishment only.

**Status:** Guard establishment
**Population:** caretaker establishment of 411
**Principal exports:** none

**Extent.** Grass across the whole habitable surface, maintained. Footpaths. Chapels at intervals for services. No town, no port beyond the receiving field, and no structure above two storeys.

**Capacity and occupancy.** Filed. Plots interred to date: 2,140,880. Plots surveyed and unallocated: 9,411,000.

**Standard.** Every person who enters Guard service records their rites at enrolment on the standard form. Cremation or interment, faith or none, is recorded there and is honoured. A standard plot is provided at no cost. Custom monument, custom plot and family attendance beyond two persons are chargeable and the schedule of charges is filed.

**Cost.** The moon is real estate in a galaxy of small and crowded worlds, given entirely to the dead. Annual maintenance appropriation, GC 4461: 8,204,600 credits. It has been granted in full in every year since establishment.
