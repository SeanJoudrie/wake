# THE BURIAL MOON
`INTACT`

Grass to the horizon, a few footpaths, and small chapels at intervals. Nobody lives here except the people who cut the grass.

**Status:** Guard establishment
**Population:** a caretaker establishment of 411
**Principal exports:** none

**What it costs.** In a galaxy of small crowded worlds, this is an entire moon given to the dead. That is the whole point and it is not sentimental: the annual maintenance appropriation is 8,204,600 credits and it has been granted in full every year since the establishment.

**The numbers.** Interred to date: 2,140,880. Surveyed and unallocated: 9,411,000.

**How you get here.** You wrote it down when you signed. Every person entering Guard service records their rites at enrolment: cremation or interment, faith or none, next of kin. A standard plot costs nothing. A custom monument, a custom plot, or more than two family members attending are chargeable, and the schedule of charges is filed.

Which means that somewhere there is a form where an eighteen-year-old ticked a box about their own body, and the Guard has kept it, and will honour it.

**When it does not work.** The establishment is built for individual services. There is one paragraph permitting a combined service at the caretakers' discretion when the numbers exceed what the moon can hold.

It has been used. The return for its use records attendance as a figure and the number of people commemorated as a range.
