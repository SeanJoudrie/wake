# CAPTAIN MEDARCO
`CONTESTED`

Does not exist.

Berji invented him at a bar, mid-impression, because the bit needed a name and that was the one that came out. He is a posture more than a person: shoulders back, chin up, the voice pitched into the flat bark of a man reading an announcement he did not write.

> *"Attention. Captain Medarco speaking. As you know, there's been way too much fun in our division."*

The impression works because everyone in the room has met him. Not him specifically. The other one, on their first posting, with a different name.
