# CARGO CULTS
`TESTIMONY`

The faiths that grew where the ships stopped coming.

**Why they are worse here.** The historical version of this happens to people who saw wealth arrive and then leave. What happened in GC 4401 was different in scale: populations that had instant medicine, interstellar travel and constant communication lost all three inside a day, and a good number of them lost every outside contact permanently.

They are not imagining a golden age. They remember it. Some of them are old enough to have used it.

**What they do.** Keep the landing grounds clear, and nothing lands. Maintain dead machines with real care. Hold observances at the feet of relay towers that no longer speak. Set goods aside against a return. Treat any surviving pre-war object as sacred rather than merely valuable, which is a distinction that stops mattering after two generations.

The characteristic object is a piece of pre-war manufacture, non-functional, whose purpose nobody alive knows, around which an entire account has grown up.

**One thing we will say plainly.** This archive has received two credible reports of communities holding ordnance in this way. We reported both. We were not told what happened next.
