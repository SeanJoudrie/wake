# CARGO CULTS
`TESTIMONY`

Practices arising among populations cut off by the Wipe, holding that the conditions of the former period will return and that their return may be prepared for or invited.

**Cause.** The fall was not gradual. Populations that had held instant medicine, interstellar transport and continuous communication lost all three within a period of days, and a proportion of them lost all outside contact permanently. What followed was not the loss of a technology but the loss of an entire order, remembered by people who had lived in it.

**Practice.** Varies. Recorded: maintenance of landing grounds that receive nothing; the ritual keeping of dead devices; observances at the sites of former relay towers; the reservation of goods against a return; and the treatment of surviving pre-war objects as sacred rather than merely valuable.

**Objects.** The characteristic object is an item of pre-war manufacture, non-functional, whose original purpose is not known to its keepers and about which an account has developed. The Restoration has recorded 88 such objects and has been permitted to examine 14.

**Note.** The Restoration has received two credible reports of communities holding ordnance in this way. We have reported both to the appropriate authorities. We have not been told what followed.
