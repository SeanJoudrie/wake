# CILLUS VOT SHAYRON-SKOLN
`INTACT`

Prince of the blood, House Shayron, Skoln line. Accredited royal press, embedded with Guard formations. One of the five named on the Sonaff assignment order.

**House register:** complete. Line, seat, maintenance, household, and every ceremonial appearance since childhood, the Royal registers being the most complete personal series in this archive.
**Accreditation:** held. It specifies what may be filmed.
**Personal effects, recorded at accreditation:** a devotional pendant. The form asks, and he answered.

**Why he left.** The register has him behind castle walls until he was not. There is no scandal in it, no debt, no marriage refused. A prince with a maintenance took a press accreditation and went out to look at the thing his family draws a revenue from.

**One filing.** The media register carries an entry for morale footage shot with a line squadron. It is logged, and then it is withdrawn from the schedule, and the withdrawal note gives no ground.

The Guard's standing order on recovered materiel permits personnel to retain what falls within a schedule. That order is catalogued separately. We put the two documents beside one another because they are dated eleven days apart and we are not able to say anything further about it.

**Present whereabouts:** `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`

The house has issued four notices of enquiry regarding his location in the past year. We hold all four. We hold no answer to any of them.
