# COMET CAPS
`RECOVERED`

A mushroom, and the reason a Guardsman keeps one eye on the calendar.

They come up in a small handful of places and travel badly, so what circulates is mostly dried. Taken properly they do what this class of thing does everywhere: the walls get interesting, the hours stop behaving, and somebody in the room announces they have worked out what everything means.

They are the running joke and the running risk on any deployment. Drug tests come round roughly every six months, which sounds generous until you realise everybody knows the date and nobody remembers it.

Legality is a local matter and a live one. Sixty-one separate drug-licensing polls have gone to the Guard franchise in the last twenty years, which means somewhere out there, a great many people have very strong feelings about a mushroom.
