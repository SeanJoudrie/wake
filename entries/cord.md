# C.O.R.D.
*Compact of Realms in Defense · vernacular: the Cord*
`RECOVERED`

Defensive bloc formed by established institutional powers in response to the spread of the Autumnal movement. Belligerent party to the Great War. Dissolved by the destruction of its constituent members.

**Formation.** The founding instrument survives in two partial copies, both fire-damaged. The recoverable text opens: "The undersigned realms, being severally threatened in their [...] and having no confidence in the sufficiency of separate defence [...]" The list of signatories is legible in nineteen places and illegible in what appears to be a further thirty to forty.

**Structure.** A compact of sovereign parties, not a state. Contributions were assessed against each member's own accounting and collected by the member itself. There is no recovered evidence of a unified command. There is a considerable quantity of recovered correspondence about establishing one.

**The Wipe.** Attributed to C.O.R.D. by the Guard and by general account. The stated reasoning, which appears in Guard educational material and in no C.O.R.D. document we hold, is that the object was to cripple Autumnal coordination. No order authorising the event has been recovered. `[NO SURVIVING PRIMARY SOURCE]`

The Restoration observes that the name is an acronym, that the Autumnals named themselves, and that this difference is remarked upon by informants across a dozen unconnected worlds without prompting.
