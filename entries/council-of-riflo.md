# THE COUNCIL OF RIFLO
`TESTIMONY`

Governing council of the settled belt, Maspin. Composition is by seat rather than by election, and the Restoration has not established how a seat is filled.

**Address.** Councillors are addressed as the Council. Names are not used and are not, so far as we can establish, recorded. Our informants were consistent that this is a rule of the body and not a courtesy.

**Seats.** Number not published. One seat is held by a representative of the Trailblazers and has been since GC 4451. That representative is human.

**Proceedings.** Catalogued under the Council Chamber. Decision is expressed through the councillors' flames. Unanimity is described as their merging to a single white.

**Competences, as reported.** Taxation and remittance to the Royal treasury. Land and concession. Labour, including the administration of indenture. Rites and observance. Relief.

**Relief.** The Council's own submissions to the Royal treasury are held here in copy and record fourteen applications for assistance over nineteen years following eruption, quake, flood and crop loss. No reply to any of them is held by this archive, and the Council states that none was received.

**Indenture.** Administered by the Council. The Restoration notes, from the Council's own registers, that indentured persons are in a number of recorded cases assigned to establishments controlled by the family of a sitting councillor. The registers record this openly, there being no provision under which it would need to be concealed.
