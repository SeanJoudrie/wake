# CRESCENT CITY
`RECOVERED`

Capital of Pashmirské, on the long peninsula that closes the northern ocean.

The peninsula is the reason the city is where it is and the reason it was fought over for as long as it was. It is approachable along one axis and is bounded by water on the rest, which made it defensible by whoever held it and expensive for whoever did not.

It changed hands during the war. How often is not agreed. The lowest figure given by any informant is four and the highest is eleven.

**Condition.** The city was never destroyed and was never repaired. What stands is a century and a half of small-arms and light ordnance damage across a fabric that was substantially complete before the fighting started, patched where a building was in use and left where it was not.

**Now.** It is the seat of the administration and holds the cabinet, the ministries and the residence. It is not a large city by the standards of this volume and it is the only one on the world with a population that has grown in each of the last returns.
