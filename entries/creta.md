# CRETA
`INTACT`

Green mountains, lakes you can see the bottom of, and spring that does not end. Four continents, four governments, and more money than any of them will discuss.

**System:** Creta (single star, nine bodies, one habitable, three worked)
**Status:** independent. Observer standing with the Guard by treaty
**Population:** between 400 million and 1.2 billion
**Membership:** open, on assimilation. Ten years resident, fluent, and the continental civil code adopted entire
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; four continental reckonings, all agricultural, all within eleven days of one another
**Principal exports:** banking, insurance, arbitration, luxury manufacture, livestock, wine

**Four countries, one policy.** The continents govern themselves and have agreed on foreign policy by permanent convention, renewed without amendment forty-one times. What they argue about instead, in the record and in public, is fishing quota, water rights, and the correct preparation of two dishes.

**Neutral, and armed about it.** Creta was not invaded, not blockaded and not bombarded, through the entire war. Its own account credits universal service sized to make occupation expensive, and a standing refusal of basing rights to anybody.

Both belligerents also banked here throughout. We record that as a second fact and not as a correction to the first.

Service is eleven months and universal. The force has no long-range transport, no expeditionary doctrine, and no legal power to acquire either.

**The vaults.** Cretan deposit records survived the Wipe because they were on paper in rooms built to survive things. The four governments state that they are complete. They are not open to this archive, to the Guard, or to anyone else. Applications are answered promptly and in the negative.

**The giving.** Endowment records are public and precise. Between GC 4403 and 4462 Cretan foundations paid for 1,206 libraries, 411 hospitals and 89 university faculties on other worlds. Where the underlying capital came from is not published.
