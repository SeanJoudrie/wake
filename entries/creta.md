# CRETA
`INTACT`

Mountainous temperate world of four continents and four governments. Banking centre. Neutral throughout the Great War and neutral since.

**System:** Creta (single star, nine bodies, one habitable, three exploited)
**Status:** independent. Not a member. Holds observer standing with the Guard by treaty
**Population:** between 400 million and 1.2 billion
**Membership:** open, on assimilation. Naturalisation requires ten years of residence, fluency, and adoption of the continental civil code in full
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; four continental reckonings, all agricultural, all within eleven days of one another
**Principal exports:** financial services, insurance, arbitration, luxury manufacture, livestock, wine

**Government.** Four continents, four separate governments, one shared external policy by permanent convention. The convention has been renewed without amendment forty-one times. Disputes between the continents in the recorded period concern fishing quota, watershed rights, and the composition of two dishes.

**Neutrality.** Maintained through the entire war. Creta was not invaded, not blockaded, and not bombarded. Its own account attributes this to a mandatory service system sized to make occupation expensive and to a standing refusal of basing rights to any party. The Restoration notes, additionally, that both belligerents held deposits there throughout.

**Service.** Universal, eleven months, all citizens. Doctrine is territorial defence. The force possesses no expeditionary capacity, no long-range transport, and no offensive doctrine, and is not permitted by its own statute to acquire any.

**Banking.** Deposit records held on Creta survived the Wipe on paper in vaults and are, by the account of the four governments, complete. They are not open to this archive, to the Guard, or to any other party. Applications for access are answered promptly and in the negative.

**Philanthropy.** Endowment records are `INTACT` and public. Between GC 4403 and GC 4462, Cretan foundations funded 1,206 libraries, 411 hospitals and 89 university faculties on other worlds, at a stated total of 14,880,300,000 credits. The sources of the underlying capital are not published.
