# DAISER
`INTACT`

Maspin. Caretaker of the temple establishment, Palace of Riflo. Indentured.

**Indenture register:** held, and complete, the Council keeping this series carefully.
**Term remaining:** seven years at the last return.
**Imputed rate:** not stated in the instrument. This is the case in 61 of the 88 indenture registers the Restoration has examined.
**Holder:** an establishment controlled by the family of a sitting councillor. The register records the relationship in the ordinary column, there being no provision requiring otherwise.
**Prior occupation:** chemist. Recorded, with a certification number.
**Off-world travel:** none recorded. No port return anywhere in our holdings carries this name.
