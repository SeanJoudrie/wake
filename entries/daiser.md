# DAISER
`INTACT`

Caretaker of the Council's temple at Riflo, and the sharpest person in the building.

He is introduced to offworlders as the Gatekeeper, which is what the job is called and not what he is. He opens the great oak doors, receives you, and shows you where to stand, and it is very easy to treat him as furniture for the first ten minutes.

> *"He's not just a gatekeeper. His name is Daiser. He's... like you. Someone surviving the pressure. Don't add more weight to his shoulders."*

A Maspin: smoke and vapour, water where the eyes should be, an ember behind it all that brightens when he is enjoying himself, which is often.

**The situation.** He is indentured. Sentenced to work off a debt that exists on paper and nowhere else, with seven years still on it and no wage to buy it out with. His holder is an establishment controlled by his own uncle, who sits on the Council. Everybody involved regards this as normal, including, most of the time, Daiser.

> *"We call it indentured servitude. Where I work off an imaginary debt until I can pay it off and be free, which is actually fucking hard without a paycheck."*

The way out is his uncle dying or a lottery that does not exist.

**Before that.** A chemist, and a properly trained one. He can make things most people cannot get, including weapons that are banned on his own world.

**How he talks.** Two registers, switched instantly. Street with anyone he likes. An elaborate, over-polished formal voice for anyone with authority, deployed with a completely straight face, which is its own kind of insult.

He has never flown. He has never been off Maspin at all. He can fly, though, and when asked where he learned he says a book or two, which is either a joke or the most alarming sentence in the temple.
