# DAN
*full name: Diontesheempluntornskalla*
`TESTIMONY`

A councillor of Riflo, and the one who will actually talk to you.

The full name is real and he does not expect anyone offworld to attempt it. Dan is what he offers, immediately, with no ceremony about it, which is exactly how Maspin names work: the long one is inherited, and the short one is the one that stuck.

**His seat is harmony.** Not defence, not trade, not rites. His job on that Council is the agreement between people, and he holds it because he believes real strength is empathy and restraint rather than the other thing.

He is the one who collects the Gift of Memory at the festival, and he takes his time about it.

> *"Wine allows humans to feel very deeply."*
