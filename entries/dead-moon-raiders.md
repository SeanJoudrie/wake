# THE DEAD MOON RAIDERS
`TESTIMONY`

An armed faction of the cold outer moons. Not a species. Composition is not established and is understood to include several populations.

**Appearance.** Consistent across every account: white or bleached cloaks over layered cold-weather dress; a black facepiece without expression, with metallic eyepieces; long rifles carried with fixed bayonets. Informants who have survived contact describe an unhurried manner of movement and use similar words for it.

**Organisation.** Unknown. No structure, no leadership, no territory and no demand has been recorded. They do not hold ground and do not appear to want it.

**Religion.** A number of the raider populations were reached by missionary orders operating out of Sonaff and hold the same faith as communities elsewhere in the sector, including communities with which they are otherwise in a state of continuous violence. At least one recorded instance exists of a raider breaking off an action on recognising a devotional pendant.

The Restoration notes that this is the only recorded circumstance under which a raider action has been observed to stop.
