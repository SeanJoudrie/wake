# THE DEAD MOON RAIDERS
`TESTIMONY`

White cloaks in a whiteout, black masks with nothing on them but metal eyepieces, long rifles with bayonets fixed.

Not a species. A faction, drawn from several populations of the cold outer moons, and beyond that nobody knows how it is put together. No structure, no leader, no territory, no demand has ever been recorded. They do not hold ground and do not appear to want any.

Everybody who has survived contact says the same thing about the way they move, which is that they are in no hurry. They come out of the storm at a walk.

**The one thing that stops them.** Missionaries reached those moons out of Sonaff, and a number of raider populations hold the same faith as communities they are otherwise permanently at war with. There is at least one recorded case of a raider breaking off in the middle of an action on seeing a devotional pendant.

Not surrender, not mercy, not law. A pendant.
