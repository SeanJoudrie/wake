# DEBT ABSOLUTION
`TESTIMONY`

A marriage between two families cancels every debt between them. Instantly, entirely, by custom, with nothing signed.

**The stated reasoning is warm.** The families are one now. A debt between them is a debt owed to yourself, and therefore meaningless.

**The practical reasoning is not.** A ruined man with a daughter and a creditor has, available to him, a single move that clears the ledger.

**Why it is not used constantly.** Because everybody can see it. It is lawful, it is free, and a family that visibly engineers one is finished socially. Not prosecuted. Finished. Everyone within a hundred miles understands exactly what was traded and for how much, and it does not get forgotten, and it attaches to the children.

So it happens rarely. And when it happens, the whole district knows within a day.

**What it produces.** A parent forbidding a good marriage because the debt is income and they would rather keep collecting. A couple genuinely in love whose wedding will look like a transaction no matter how they conduct themselves. Somebody borrowing deliberately, at ruinous rates, to force a family's hand. And, most often, a spouse who is loved perfectly well and will never once be certain whether they were chosen or written off.

In a post-war economy where debt is the ordinary condition and collection has been contracted out to people with weapons, the custom is everywhere and is deeply, quietly resented.
