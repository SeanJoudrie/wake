# THE DESTRUCTION OF WAKE
`CONTESTED`

The most talked-about event in living memory, and the one nobody can agree on.

**What the Guard says.** One ordnance deployment, Autumnal forces. Over in under an hour. Responsibility stated flatly, with no source cited and no unit named. It appears as settled fact in every piece of Guard educational material.

**What the people say.** We collected thirty-eight accounts across eleven settlements and six worlds, all of them from people who were children then or repeating a parent. Every single one describes something that took days. Two to nine of them. Six say the sky changed colour before anything was heard. Four describe evacuation orders. One describes those orders being cancelled.

An hour and nine days are not two versions of one event.

We note, and draw nothing from it, that the account was published by the party that later received the exclusive licence to mine the remains, and that there is nobody left on the other side to answer it.

**How many died.** Between 900 million and 4 billion. Neither number rests on a document. Both rest on people.

**[SPOILER] It was arranged.** The Nhath were hired to do it. The Guard contracted them with plausible deniability so that the refined fuel could be sold to a middleman rather than paid for, because paying for it would have meant paying the extraction families their completed term.

The raid was not a raid. It was a way of closing an account.

**[SPOILER] What that explains.** The attack fell inside the two week window when the station had no radio. Mr Armstin's parts never arrived, while the candy did. The rescue that was not coming arrived a day later anyway.

None of those were coincidences and none of them were failures. They were the schedule.
