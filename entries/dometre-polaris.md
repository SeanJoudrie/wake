# PRINCE DOMETRÉ POLARIS
`CONTESTED`

A prince of the blood who appeared once, in one room, agreed to terms on behalf of a Royal house, and cannot be found in any register anywhere.

**What is on the record.** He was received in full session at the Council Chamber of Riflo and addressed by the centre councillor as *Prince Dometré Polaris, negotiator for the House of Kafkee, Flamebearer.* The Council took the minute in its own hand and in its own script, as it does, and has never amended it.

> *"May your shadow burn long."*

**The terms he agreed.** Ten million in Royal scrip, against long neglect. One unit of Royal soldiers, to hold the shipping lanes against piracy. And a Gift of Memory.

The first two are ordinary instruments. The third is not something a Royal house has ever been asked for, and there is no precedent anywhere in this archive for a crown party accepting one.

**The problem with the name.** Polaris and Kafkee are separate houses. A Polaris carrying a Kafkee ceremonial office is not forbidden and is also not filed, anywhere, in any register we hold, for anybody. The Royal lists carry no Dometré. They also carry a very great many thin-blooded claimants who are entitled to a seat and a small maintenance and appear on no list at all, which is precisely why this entry is contested rather than closed.

**What the room made of him.** Not much, at first. One elder stated she had already seen his face, in a vision, kneeling, in the posture of a servant. Another observed that his father had stood on the same floor and lied without his flame so much as flickering. He was tested on songcraft, on the ground that the Royals were once said to sing at every rite, and an exemption was produced on his behalf from the Elder Harmonists.

> *"Your tongue wears the sand of other stars, boy. Not Polaris rain."*

Then his pendant answered theirs, and the room changed. What was said afterwards is recorded as *a rare sign, for the family,* and as *sacred, he may be.*

**The beard.** He wore one. The Royal court requires its household and its blood to go clean-shaven, by long ceremonial rule, and has for as long as there has been a court. Nobody in the chamber raised it. Everybody outside it has.

**Since.** No soldiers arrived. No payment was made. The Council has not withdrawn the minute, has not written to any house, and declines to discuss the matter with this archive.

**[SPOILER] Who he is.** There is no such person. The name is a false identity carried by Pavlock Jattern, built with Calsco Fritt, in order to be admitted to the Council Chamber of Riflo as an envoy of House Kafkee.

Every commitment recorded under the name was made by a Guard combat engineer with no authority to make it. The archive's inability to find the man is not a failure of the record.

*[Compiler's note: a man walked into the one room on that world where a decision happens, gave a name, and left with an agreement. We can tell you what he promised to the credit. We cannot tell you that he exists.]*
