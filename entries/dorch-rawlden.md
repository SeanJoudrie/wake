# DORCH RAWLDEN
`TESTIMONY`

Son of Grai Penston Rawlden, aged about twenty-three. He holds a junior post in the municipal administration of Crescent City and has held no other.

**Standing.** He was born outside marriage, at a time when his father was already a senior figure, and to Entonia Yalenti, a journalist working within the limits the statutes allow, who had been attached to his father when both were young, and who is dead. She died of a cancer attributed to industrial contamination in the town where she and Rawlden were both raised. Rawlden has the same disease. The administration promotes monogamy as stated policy on the grounds that it is stabilising, and he is the publicly known exception to it.

He carries his father's name. On a world where children born outside a marriage frequently do not, this is generally read as an acknowledgement rather than as a courtesy, and it is the only formal thing anybody can point to in his position. No instrument defines his standing and no office is reserved for him.

**The post.** Dorch is responsible for scheduling refuse collection across several districts of the capital. It is unimportant work, it is done adequately, and he applied for it himself against open advertisement.

Informants who deal with the administration are consistent that he is given nothing of consequence, and equally consistent that this is not a punishment or a precaution. He has never asked for anything of consequence. Several describe him as tired in a way that is unusual at his age.

**What he is reported to want.** To live offworld under his own name and be no relation to anybody. This is said by people who know him and is not said by him in any account held here.

**Why he has not gone.** He is frightened. Of the galaxy, from what reaches him about it, and by several accounts of his own world as well.

He is also, so far as this archive can establish, the one person on Pashmirské who could leave without asking. Everybody else who has departed in the last decade was put on a ship and told not to return.

**Sources.** Four informants, three of them former municipal employees and one deported under the second chance. None has described a conversation with him lasting more than a few minutes.
