# DORROW
`INTACT`

The mills. Grain arrives raw from Weton and leaves as flour, oil and cake, and the difference between those two states is where the money in this system is made.

**System:** Polaris
**Status:** local government retained. Licensing held on Kelton
**Population:** between 4 million and 25 million
**Calendars in use:** Polaris Harvest Reckoning
**Principal exports:** flour, oil, meal, cake

**Throughput, :** 1,188,400,000 tonnes in. 1,141,220,000 tonnes of finished product out.

The processing margin is the largest single margin in the system. It is realised on Kelton, where the product is sold, and not here, where it is made.

Dorrow has the second-highest output value of the ten inhabited bodies and the second-lowest household income.
