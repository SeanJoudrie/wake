# DORROW
`INTACT`

Worked moon of the Polaris system. Milling and threshing.

**System:** Polaris
**Status:** local government retained. Licensing held on Kelton
**Population:** between 4 million and 25 million
**Membership:** application and assessment
**Calendars in use:** Polaris Harvest Reckoning
**Principal exports:** flour, oil, meal, cake

Grain arrives from Weton raw and leaves Dorrow as flour, oil and cake. Filed throughput GC 4461: 1,188,400,000 tonnes in, 1,141,220,000 tonnes of finished product out.

**Value.** The processing margin is the largest single margin in the system. It is realised on Kelton, where the product is sold, and not on Dorrow, where it is created. Dorrow's mean household income is the second lowest of the ten inhabited bodies. Its filed output value is the second highest.
