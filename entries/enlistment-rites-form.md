# THE ENLISTMENT RITES FORM
`INTACT`

Six questions you answer on your first day, about your own body.

Burned or buried. Which faith, from a list of eighty-eight, with a blank line if yours is not on it. Whether your family wants you sent home at their own cost. Two names. Your signature. The date.

You cannot start without filling it in, and once filed it is kept forever, because decades later it is the document somebody reads to find out what to do with you.

It can be changed at any time by writing in. Fewer than two people in a hundred ever do.

*[Compiler's note: it is a good form. It is well drafted and it is honoured. The volunteer transcribing the GC 4444 series stopped for the day when she noticed what the ages in the third column were.]*
