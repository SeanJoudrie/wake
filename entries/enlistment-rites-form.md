# THE ENLISTMENT RITES FORM
`INTACT`

Standard form completed on entry into Guard service. Records the person's funerary rites.

**Content.** Six questions. Cremation or interment. Faith, or none, from a schedule of 88 with provision for a written entry. Whether remains are to be repatriated at family expense. Two named next of kin. A signature. A date.

**Completion.** Universal. The form is part of the enrolment pack and service does not commence without it. Forms are filed by service number and are retained permanently, being the instrument on which the entitlement is later discharged.

**Age at completion.** Ordinary entrants complete the form at the standard age of contract. Wardship entrants complete it on transition from the Academy to service.

**Amendment.** Permitted at any time on written application. Filed amendment rate: 1.9 per cent.
