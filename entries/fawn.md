# FAWN
`RECOVERED`

Three shipping schedules, one insurance schedule, and a man who used to drive supplies out there.

Nothing else. No survey. No population figure. No accession instrument. Nobody in this archive has been.

**What can be said.** It takes deliveries. Somebody organises convoys out to it, which means there are people there who need feeding and equipment that needs parts, and the routes are difficult enough that leading them counts as a line on a service record.

That is the whole of it. A place that exists because things are sent to it, known to the galaxy as a destination on a manifest and to almost nobody as a place.
