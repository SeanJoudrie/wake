# FLOOMS
`TESTIMONY`

Native fauna of Maspin. Kept, herded and managed rather than farmed. Regarded locally as the basis of the world's ecology.

**Behaviour.** Nervous, affectionate, and strongly responsive to the disposition of persons around them. Every informant, without exception, described flooms as forming rapid and durable judgements about individual people, and several described those judgements as reliable. The Restoration records this as reported.

**Ecological function.** Flooms absorb ambient biological energy in a manner informants compare to a plant taking light. They lay unfertilised eggs, filled with a blue fluid, on dry ground and failing growth. Recovery of the ground follows within approximately one week and is visible in the Trailblazer concession's own soil returns, which record no imported fertiliser and rising yields.

The local formulation, given to us in the same words by three informants on separate occasions: floom is the ecosystem, all things live because of flooms, and floom lives because of life.

**Product.** The egg fluid is a medicine of exceptional potency. Catalogued separately.

**Trade.** Flooms are exported. The export licences are filed and the numbers are small. The Council restricts the trade and has refused three applications to expand it.
