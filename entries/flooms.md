# FLOOMS
`TESTIMONY`

The creature Maspin runs on, and the reason the air there is as good as it is.

They are nervous, affectionate, and much smarter than they look. Everyone who has kept them says the same thing, unprompted: a floom decides very quickly what it thinks of a person, and it is almost never wrong. People on Maspin take this seriously enough to act on it.

**What they do.** They take in ambient life energy the way a plant takes light. Not stealing it, not draining anything, just standing in it.

Then they lay eggs on dead ground: unfertilised, full of a blue liquid, dropped on dry grass and failing gardens. About a week later the ground has come back greener than it was. The Trailblazer concession imports no fertiliser at all and its yields keep climbing.

The local phrasing, given to us word for word by three different people:

> *Floom is the ecosystem. All things live because of flooms, and floom lives because of life.*

**Trade.** They are exported, in very small numbers. The Council restricts it and has turned down three applications to expand it.
