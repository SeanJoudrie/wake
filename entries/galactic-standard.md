# GALACTIC STANDARD
`INTACT`

The common language of trade, law and administration. Spoken as a first language by the great majority of human populations and as a second language nearly everywhere else.

**Status.** Not imposed by any instrument. It is the language of the code, of the ledger, of shipping and of the Guard, and this has been sufficient.

**Uniformity.** High and rising. The educational material of the member worlds is produced centrally. Comparison of the recovered pre-war regional forms with current speech shows the loss of a substantial number of local features within two generations.

**Displacement.** The Restoration's survey records 61 languages and dialects attested in living memory with no living speaker recorded at the time of survey. The rate of loss is highest on worlds where education was transferred to sponsored foundations under contract, and the correlation is strong enough that we note it while observing that we have not established a cause.

**Provision for other languages.** None in the code. Galactic Standard is the language of proceedings and translation is at the party's own expense, save in criminal matters, where it is provided.
