# THE GASHI
`INTACT`

Green-skinned, and nobody can agree on whether that skin is reptile or amphibian.

Up close it settles the question no better. It has the fine dry grain of scale in some lights and the give of something that would rather be damp in others, and the Gashi themselves find the question tedious in the way any people find it tedious when strangers open with it.

**What they wear.** Robes and tunics, cut long, and over them a great deal of decorative gear that is built to move: panels, sashes, hanging lengths of fabric weighted at the ends so they swing and settle rather than hang dead. A Gashi walking across a room is doing something that a human walking across a room is not.

**The hair.** All colours, every variety, and no two families the same. What is consistent is the quality of it. Gashi hair grows thick, holds a shape, takes a comb, and looks in every case as though it has just been finished by somebody very good, which is not a thing they work at and is very annoying to everyone else.

**What they do.** Artisans, overwhelmingly, in the broad sense rather than the narrow one. Chefs, painters, teachers, engravers, people who make the small good version of a thing that could have been made badly and cheaply instead. It is a whole world, so it is also farmers and clerks and mechanics, but the culture is built around art and sophistication and it judges accordingly.

Which makes the ones who go into other lines of work interesting, because the sophistication does not come off. It just gets applied to something else.
