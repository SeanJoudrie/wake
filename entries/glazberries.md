# GLAZBERRIES
`INTACT`

Small teal berries with a hard glossy skin, bitter enough raw to make you pull a face, and good cooked or fermented.

They grow wild on several jungle worlds and are farmed properly on Kinton. Their real distinction is that almost everybody can eat them: they agree with humans and with most surveyed physiologies, which is rare enough that three separate services put them in the standard field ration.
