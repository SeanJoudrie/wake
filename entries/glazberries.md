# GLAZBERRIES
`INTACT`

Small teal fruit with a hard glossy skin. Bitter raw and sweet when cooked or fermented.

Native to several jungle worlds and cultivated on Kinton. Edible to humans and to most surveyed physiologies, which is unusual and is the reason it appears in the standard field ration schedule of three separate services.
