# GRAVES
`RECOVERED`

The oldest of the five sent to Sonaff, which is why he ended up in charge of them.

Nobody appointed him. There were five people in a blizzard outside a sealed hangar, not one of them infantry, and he was the one who had been in longest, so he gave the orders.

> *"Plan? Stay in position, shoot anything you see that isn't me."*

That is the entire plan, and given what he had to work with it is not a bad one.
