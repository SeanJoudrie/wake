# GRAVITY SPIRES
`INTACT`

Towers that burn Corefuel to hold an atmosphere onto a rock that has no business keeping one.

A spire generates a local gravitational field and a pressure envelope around itself, about 1.4 kilometres across. Put three to seven of them in a cluster with overlapping envelopes and you can live on an asteroid: walk outside, plant a garden, hang washing.

They are how the Wake fragment colonies existed at all. The homeworld's own core, refined, keeping the people standing on its wreckage alive.

**What breaks.** The regulator assembly, always. Nobody can make one; they come out of salvage and pre-war stock, issued quarterly against a settlement's entitlement.

**What happens if a cluster goes.** The envelope opens over four to eleven hours depending on its size. The safety schedule says get to a hardened structure. It does not say what to do after that.
