# GRAVITY SPIRES
`INTACT`

Fixed installations that generate a local gravitational field and retain an atmosphere on bodies too small to hold one. Powered by Corefuel. In use across asteroid habitation, low-mass moons, and orbital agricultural platforms.

**Coverage.** A standard spire holds an envelope of approximately 1.4 kilometres radius at habitable pressure. Fragment settlements are typically served by a cluster of three to seven, spaced so that failure of one does not open the envelope.

**Maintenance.** The regulator assembly is the failure point. It is not manufacturable. Replacement regulators come from salvage and from pre-war stock, and are issued against a quarterly parts entitlement tied to the installation's licensing class.

**Failure.** Documented failure of a full cluster produces atmosphere loss over a period given in the safety schedule as four to eleven hours depending on envelope volume. The schedule specifies evacuation to a hardened structure and does not specify what to do after that.
