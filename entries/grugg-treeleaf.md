# GRUGG TREELEAF
`RECOVERED`

Carried in unconscious, admitted under a name he did not give, and that is the name he has now.

**What happened.** A medevac out of jungle country on Maspin, with three men in it and one of them past speaking. Somebody else supplied his name on the radio. The base took it down, the medbay admitted him under it, and every subsequent line of his treatment, his theatre time and his discharge sits in that file under that name.

He was not asked and could not have answered. By the time anybody could have corrected it, it had been written eleven times.

**The name itself.** *Treeleaf* is a Maspin surname in the ordinary Maspin pattern, which is to say a tree, taken by the household that raised you. It is unremarkable and it is local.

*Grugg* is not a Maspin word, is not a Maspin sound, and does not occur anywhere else in our material.

So the name is half of one world and half of nowhere, joined at a medevac, and there is no arrangement under which a person would have arrived at it themselves.

**Condition on admission.** The record is specific about the injury and vague about the man. Facial. Extensive. He lived.

**Since.** No follow-up, no transfer, no next of kin contacted, and no correction ever filed. The file is closed and correct and in that name.

*[Compiler's note: three men gave three names on one call and only one of them was in a condition to be lying. This archive is a great many files exactly like this one, and we normally do not notice, because normally there is nobody left to be wrong about.]*
