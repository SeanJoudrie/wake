# GUARD NICKNAMES
`INTACT`

Nobody in this service is called what is on their file, and how many things a man is called tells you exactly how many people are close enough to take a liberty with him.

**Nobody agrees, and the disagreement is the whole system.** A name does not shorten once. It shortens differently in every direction, and the versions do not compete or resolve. One staff sergeant in the personnel series is attested, in the same year, as Jim, Jimzy, Jimz, Jimzo, Jimbo and Jimmy. Six people, six words, one man, and not one of them adopted anybody else's.

Which means the nickname is not really about him. It is a record of a relationship, held by the other person.

**Who is allowed.** Anybody who has earned it, and there is no ceremony for earning it. What there is instead is a very reliable tell: the first time somebody shortens your name in front of the room and nobody looks up.

**The full name is the distance.** Read the establishment by this and it goes the wrong way from rank. A Special Operations sergeant has six names. A colonel of the covert division has a rank and a surname. The men in this volume who are only ever given one name, in full, every time, are the men nobody has ever been in a room with twice.

**Getting it wrong on purpose.** A recognised move, and not an affectionate one. You mangle a man's name, hold his eye, and wait to see whether he corrects you. Correcting is not the loss. The loss is the pause before you decide.

Some people never correct anybody, ever, about anything. It is worth knowing which ones.

**Short forms that stopped being short.** Yakib became Yake so completely that both turn up in the same conversation in the same mouth, and the man answers to either without noticing there were two.

**Elsewhere.** The Maspin do the same thing and made it the law: the inherited name is long and the one that stuck is the one you give a stranger. Nhathian fuses a three-part name to two syllables for the people who love him and keeps eleven for anybody taking a statement.

Three peoples, no contact, one arrangement. Nobody planned it and it is not in any instrument.

*[Compiler's note: the enlistment form has one line for a name. We hold four thousand two hundred and six testimonies and have not once heard a soldier use what is on it.]*
