# GUARD PAY
`INTACT`

It is a very good living, and that is the point of it.

A Spec Ops captain draws about 110,000 credits a year. None of it is taxed by anybody, because no member world has the standing to assess a Guard contract and the Guard does not assess itself. His meals are made for him. His quarters cost him nothing. Whatever he retains under Standing Order 41 at a cleared objective is his and is entered nowhere, and men at that level do not lose much of what they pick up.

So the money simply accumulates. Ten years of that is a million credits, and a career is twenty.

**What the money is not.** It is not freedom. He is inside a fixed term and cannot go where he likes, cannot decline the work that comes, and cannot stop being where the Guard has put him. He is extremely well paid and he is not at liberty, and those two things sit together comfortably enough that most people stop noticing.

**What it looks like from the other side of the ledger.** A single Castle Team operation takes down a cartel principal, or ends a criminal system, or hands control of a whole system to somebody who will pay for it. The Guard books that against a salary of 110,000 a year.

A million credits over a decade, against what a decade of that produces, is not generosity. It is the best purchase in the accounts.

**And the term that follows from it.** The contract is a performance instrument. It pays because the man is effective. Somebody who stops being effective stops being a good line in the ledger and becomes a problem to be resolved, and the resolution is arrived at the way every other decision here is arrived at, on paper, by people who have never met him. Nothing about it is personal. That is the part that should worry you.
