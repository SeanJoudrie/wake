# GUARD QUARTERS
`INTACT`

One room, one bed, one door, and the same box for everybody at that level.

Spec Ops personnel get a single cabin to themselves. Not a suite, not a set of rooms, and a captaincy does not enlarge it. The Guard's view of accommodation is that a bed is a bed and the difference between people is expressed in the pay, not the floor plan.

What the rooms do differ in is everything else.

**One of them is bare.** Nothing on the walls. The bed made properly every morning, tight, out of habit rather than inspection. On the surface there is a grey stone from a road on a world that no longer exists, worn smooth from twenty years of being turned over in a hand, and for a while there was a ring in a velvet box. That is the entire contents. A man can live in a room for eight years and leave nothing in it that would tell you his name.

**One of them is full.** Same cabin, same dimensions, same standard-issue bed. Lights strung along the walls. Posters. Plants, actual living ones, which on a ship is a decision that has to be made repeatedly rather than once.

The room the team gathers in is the second one. Nobody ever decided that either.
