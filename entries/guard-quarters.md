# GUARD QUARTERS
`INTACT`

One room, one bed, one door, and the same box for everybody at that level.

Spec Ops personnel get a single cabin to themselves. Not a suite, not a set of rooms, and a captaincy does not enlarge it. Offered upgraded quarters on the top floor or beside the Captain's Club, at least one man has turned both down on the grounds that everything he owns fits in a backpack.

What the rooms differ in is everything else.

**One of them is empty on purpose.** Immaculate, organised past the point of tidiness and into starkness. Nothing on the walls. A bed made properly every morning. A functional desk. Two plants on the windowsill under the soft glow of a lamp, which are the only living things in the room and are kept alive with the same discipline as everything else.

On the desk there is a grey stone off a colony road, which for years sat there in solitude, and for a few weeks sat beside a small velvet box. That is the whole inventory. A man can live in a room for eight years and leave nothing in it that would tell you his name.

The closet holds a crisp blue polo with the trident on the chest. Behind the door, on a bad week, an astrovin bottle with a few inches left in it, and black paint on the desk from a helmet repainted by hand at three in the morning.

**One of them is full.** Same cabin, same dimensions, same standard-issue bed.

Strands of light snake along the walls, flickering between blue and purple and green. Stolen and scavenged logos hang as faint holographic projections. A ceiling projector turns a slow aurora across the ceiling in violet and emerald, and the floor shifts colour under every step. The lighting is dim, and all of it is deliberate.

The shelves are a museum of Standing Order 41: defunct pistols with cracked barrels, scavenged gears, gold chains, battle-worn foreign helmets, and a few dented ashtrays still dusted with redleaf. There is an enormous beanbag in one corner. The whole room smells of synthetic flowers doing a poor job of covering the earthy musk of redleaf smoke, which has got into the furniture and is not coming out.

Cluttered, but lived in. Messy, but intentional.

The room the team gathers in is the second one. Nobody ever decided that either.
