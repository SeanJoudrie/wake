# GUARD RATIONS
`INTACT`

What you eat when you are not in the dining hall, which is most of the time.

**The good one.** Meal 25, the Brellovine pasta kit. It heats properly, it tastes like something a person made, and it is the currency of every field kitchen in the Guard. Men will trade three of anything for one.

**The other ones.** The Tipa sandwich, which is fine, which is the most anybody has ever said about it. Frozen guomo sticks. And the protein wafer: dry, nearly flavourless, sold by the sleeve at every commissary, and the single most stolen item in the establishment.

The wafers are not stolen because anybody wants them. They are stolen because they are the easiest thing to take, and because a cadet who hands them out behind a latrine wall to four other children is not distributing food. He is distributing the fact that he did something.

**Off the supply chain.** Anywhere with a market, the good stuff is local: rockroot by the sack, glazberries, spice bundles tied with string, weeflowers for somebody's wife, charcandies for somebody's children. Yinmelon sauce comes in a can heavy enough that nobody picks one up expecting it to be light.

**And what a colony eats.** Seared tofu. Mashed rockroot. Spireleafs on the side, which no child on any world has ever finished voluntarily.
