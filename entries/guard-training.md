# GUARD TRAINING
`INTACT`

There are two of these and they are not the same experience at all.

**The short one.** Sixteen-year-olds off poor worlds with no schooling behind them and no particular aptitude in front of them, run through in the minimum time the schedule allows. They are taught to hold a rifle, follow an order, and stand where they are put. It is ordinary infantry training and it produces ordinary infantry, and the Guard needs a great many of them and is not pretending otherwise.

**The long one**, which is most of the establishment and everything above it.

Sleep deprivation as a working condition rather than an incident. Being told, continuously and by people who are good at saying it, that you are nothing. Physical work past the point where the body has stopped cooperating. And the arrangement that does the actual damage: at every hour of it there is an invitation to stop, and no mechanism that lets you.

You can want to quit as much as you like. Wanting is free. There is nowhere to hand the wanting in.

That is the whole design. They are not trying to find out whether you can be made to want to leave, because everybody can. They are trying to find out what you do for eleven more weeks once you do.

**Afterwards.** Nobody who came out of it discusses it much, and the people who came out of the short one are aware that there was another door.
