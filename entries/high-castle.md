# THE HIGH CASTLE
`INTACT`

The crown's house on Shayron. Four wings around a court, raised across two centuries in four separate building campaigns, none of which matched the last.

The bones of it are Maldo steel, shipped in from the old capital while that world still had an industry. The eastern towers are the oldest section standing and carry more of the original metal than anything else in the building.

That matters more than it sounds. Maldo ore is worked out. There is no more of that steel anywhere, so every repair since has been made in some other alloy that does not quite match, and the maintenance records track the building as a falling percentage of itself.

The court and the western wing open to the public eleven days a year. The rest is the household's.
