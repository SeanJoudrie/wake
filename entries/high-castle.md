# THE HIGH CASTLE
`INTACT`

Principal Royal residence and ceremonial seat, Shayron. Seat of the crown. Not a seat of government.

**Fabric.** Four principal wings about a court, raised in four campaigns over 211 years. Structural members throughout are Maldo steel, shipped from the old capital during and after the period of its industry. The eastern towers are the earliest surviving section and carry the greatest proportion of original material.

**Insurance.** The fabric, the fittings, and the regalia are insured. The schedule of insured items is `INTACT` and itemised, running to 14,206 entries with a valuation against each. It is the most detailed inventory of any structure in this archive.

**Material note.** The steel cannot be replaced. Maldo ore is exhausted. Any part of this building that is lost can be rebuilt and cannot be rebuilt out of the same thing.

**Access.** The court and the western wing are open to the public on eleven days of the year. Attendance is ticketed and filed.
