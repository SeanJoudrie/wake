# HOLOCLOAKS
`CONTESTED`

Not invisibility. Something worse to be on the wrong end of.

Activated from the gauntlet, the armour's contours dissolve into a rippling distortion and the light bends around the wearer. What is left is a whisper of heat-shimmer moving across a landscape, indistinguishable from the landscape unless you already know to look.

**What gives it away.** Footprints. The system does not hide the ground, and on ash or sand or snow the prints are perfectly visible for as long as it takes the wind to take them. Standing still, a man in a holocloak is a rumour. Moving, he is a trail.

**The gauge.** There is a power meter on the gauntlet and it is the only thing that matters. It runs down fast, it does not recover in the field, and every operator who has ever worn one has spent the entire approach doing arithmetic against it.

Restricted issue, and not manufactured anywhere the archive can name.
