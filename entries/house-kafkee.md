# HOUSE KAFKEE
`RECOVERED`

Royal house of middle standing. Holds the ceremonial office rendered as Flamebearer.

The house register is `RECOVERED` and partial. Line, seats, and maintenance are legible. The endowment schedule is not. `[RECORD DAMAGED]`

The office of Flamebearer is attested in the court ceremonial as a bearer of light at investiture and at rites of the crown. Its duties beyond that are not recorded in any document held here, and the Restoration is unable to say whether the office presently carries any.
