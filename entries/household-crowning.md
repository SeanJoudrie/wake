# HOUSEHOLD CROWNING
`TESTIMONY`

The couple are crowned. Not figuratively.

**The rite.** Two crowns, held above the heads of the pair or set on them outright, and from that moment they are sovereign over their own household. It is stated in exactly those terms. Their home is their territory, their decisions within it are theirs, and no one outside it holds authority there.

The crowns themselves are usually family property, worn by a great many heads across a great many generations, and are frequently not worth very much.

**Why it works where it works.** On worlds where an actual crown sits an hour's walk away in an actual palace, the rite is not modest. It is a claim. Every married couple on that world has been sovereign of something, briefly, in front of witnesses, and the distance between them and the family in the castle is a matter of scale rather than of kind.

Nobody in authority has ever objected, because objecting would require saying out loud what the objection is.

**And there is a joke in it that the Royals do not always hear.** The house that holds the throne came up from metalworkers. The galaxy's most exalted family and the couple crowned in a kitchen with two dented heirlooms are, at the origin, in the same trade.
