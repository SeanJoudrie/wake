# HOUSING
`INTACT`

Buying is the easy part. Keeping the roof on is the problem.

There is no galactic housing system and there never was. Some worlds run housing blocks. Some worlds you can buy a house outright for very little. Some worlds bury the whole business in paperwork and some have none at all. The honest answer to how a person gets a home is the answer it has always been: you get a job, and you get a bad house or a good one.

**What is different here** is that a building is not a thing you own. It is a thing you are maintaining, against a supply chain that no longer exists.

The structure is fine. Structures last. It is the regulator, the seal, the pump, the coupling, the part in the middle of the thing, and none of it is made any more. It comes out of salvage or pre-war stock or it does not come at all, and a person can spend nineteen consecutive quarters filing correct requests for a part that is simply gone.

**So the shape of a home is the shape of its repairs.** Patched roofs. A window that was replaced with something that was not a window. Improvised fittings, done well, by somebody who apprenticed for a year and worked the rest out. Whole streets where every house has the same failure at a different stage.

**And a world can lose.** Not to a war or a disaster. Just to arithmetic, quietly, over decades, as the parts run out faster than they can be found, until the roofs are open and there are cathedrals with weather coming through them and children being taught properly in a building that is losing.

Crowded apartments, everywhere, on nearly every world. Busy-crowded rather than desperate-crowded. People manage.
