# INDENTURED SERVITUDE
`INTACT`

Labour held against a debt. Lawful under Galactic Standard law. Practised on 44 of the jurisdictions holding a slave-free declaration.

**Form.** A person is bound to work for a holder until a stated sum is discharged. The obligation is to the debt and not to the holder, which is the distinction on which its lawfulness rests. The debt may be original, assigned, inherited, or imposed by a court in place of a custodial sentence.

**Discharge.** Where the indentured person receives no wage, the debt is discharged by imputed value of labour at a rate set by the holder or by local statute. The Restoration has examined 88 indenture registers. In 61 of them the imputed rate is not stated in the instrument.

**Duration.** Recorded terms in our sample range from 11 months to 31 years. The median is 9 years. Terms may be extended on stated grounds, and the grounds are set out in the local statute rather than in the instrument.

**Administration.** By local authority. On Maspin, by the Council of Riflo. The Restoration notes from the Council's own registers that indentured persons are in a number of cases assigned to establishments controlled by the family of a sitting councillor, and that the registers record this without qualification, there being no provision requiring otherwise.

**Guard position.** The Guard has stated in successive ministerial material that the practice is to be ended, and that the priority is first the completion of membership and the establishment of a uniform register, after which measures will follow. This position has been stated in materially identical terms in GC 4429, 4437, 4448 and 4459.
