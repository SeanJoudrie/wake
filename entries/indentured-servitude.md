# INDENTURED SERVITUDE
`INTACT`

Working off a debt. Legal everywhere, including on the worlds that advertise themselves as slave-free.

The distinction that makes it lawful is that you are not owned. You owe. The debt can be one you took on, one somebody assigned to you, one you inherited, or one a court handed you instead of a prison sentence.

**How you get out.** You do not get paid, so the debt comes down by the value of your labour at a rate somebody else sets. We looked at eighty-eight indenture registers. In sixty-one of them the instrument does not state the rate at all.

Recorded terms run from eleven months to thirty-one years. The middle of that is nine.

**Who runs it.** Whoever the local authority is. On Maspin that is the Council of Riflo, whose own registers show indentured people assigned to households belonging to sitting councillors, written down plainly in the ordinary column, because nothing requires anyone to hide it.

**What the Guard says.** That it is going to be ended, and that the priority is first finishing membership and building a uniform register, after which measures will follow. That position has been published in materially identical words in GC 4429, 4437, 4448 and 4459.
