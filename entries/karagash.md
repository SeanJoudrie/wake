# KARAGASH
`RECOVERED`

A Gashi bounty hunter, and the most reasonably priced frightening thing in the sector.

Seven feet. Strong through the shoulders and the arms without any of the bulk that usually comes attached, so the size does not register properly until he is standing next to something you know the height of. His hand closes on a wrist like a vise closing, and he is fast in a way that people who have watched it are not able to describe afterwards without their voice changing.

**The face.** He wears a mask across the lower half of it, a breathing apparatus rather than a disguise, and it has been there long enough that the shape of him without it is not a thing many people have seen. Above it, gold and black eyes with no pupil anybody has ever managed to find. You can tell exactly when he looks at something, because the light comes off them differently when they move.

**The rest.** Black suits, cut for a human frame and made to fit his, and he wears them well. A bandolier across the chest, which is not strictly necessary and is clearly a thing he enjoys. And ginger hair, deep and well combed and frankly gorgeous, which on a Gashi is normal and on a seven-foot masked man in a black suit is the detail people bring up first.

**The work.** He hunts for hire. He is extremely good at it and he does not extend mercy, and he is also not angry, ever, about any part of it. The two usually travel together and in him they simply do not. He takes the job, states the price, does the job, collects, and wishes you a good one.

Nobody knows why he went into this line rather than one of the ten thousand respectable ones a Gashi is expected to want. There is speculation and none of it is worth repeating. The likeliest answer is the one that never gets proposed, which is that he tried it and liked it.

**And one detail nobody can account for.** He works out of a spool of equipment carried at the hip. On the single occasion anybody got a proper look at what came off it, it was a length of green rubber garden hose, torn out of a wall spigot by hand and still clumped with wet dirt.

He offered no explanation. He was not asked twice.

He is, for what it is worth, modestly priced.

**[SPOILER] The garden hose again.** It reappears, and the second time it is bait. He carries it coiled like a heavy spool of cable while Jimzon plays an irritated foreman, which holds a guard's attention long enough for the guard to walk into the alley.

The guard works out what the spool actually is about one second before Calsco lands behind him.

**[SPOILER] Who he works for.** Quinn. After Cillus, the two of them work bounties together, and Karagash is the one standing behind him when he walks into a room he has not been invited into.
