# KASPIAN
*Maspin name: Kadam*
`CONTESTED`

Older than the species that names him, and older than the one that made it.

**What he was.** One of the people who lived on Wake before humans, back when it was a world and not a debris field. His remains went down with it: a skull, somewhere under what used to be the mantle, now scattered with the rest of it across a stretch of empty sky.

**What he is.** A skull pulled up out of desert sand, wreathed in a thin purple flame that defines its edges rather than consuming them. Lightning comes down to it slowly, like honey rather than a strike, and the sound it makes is a ceremonial drum instead of thunder. A translucent body of shifting starlight phases into place around it, tall and unhurried, with two points of gold where the eyes belong.

**On Maspin they call him Kadam,** and he is carved into the great gold doors of the Palace of Riflo: an elongated figure hovering just off the ground, arms out, handing a small burning sun down to the Maspins kneeling below. It is a copy of something much older, polished and re-gilded by every generation that inherits it.

Ask a Maspin what it means and you get *knowledge*, or *power*, or a shrug. Ask a shaman where they learned what they know, and the answer is him. Ask whether he is real, and even they are not sure.

**The name is not his.** Asked what he is called, he says he has never had a name in the sense other people have them, and that his people are called Kaspian. Everybody since has used it as though it were his, including this volume, because there has never been anything else to use.

**One line is recorded.** Nobody has been able to tell us what it refers to.

> *The queen will return to many moons.*
