# KAZ
`CONTESTED`

A bounty hunter, and a voice giving orders in a room the record does not describe.

The trade he is in is an old one and a legal one in most of the places it operates, which is why it has stayed as big as it has. Somebody wants a person found, the price is agreed, and no part of the arrangement requires anybody to explain themselves to a government. There are Guard warrants that get worked this way. There are also plenty that do not.

Of Kaz specifically there is very little: no world, no species, no description, no list of jobs. He works with at least one other person, whom he addresses by name and does not appear to entirely trust.
