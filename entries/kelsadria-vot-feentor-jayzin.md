# KELSADRIA VOT FEENTOR-JAYZIN
`INTACT`

Royal descent, House Feentor, Jayzin line. Guard flight personnel.

**House register:** held. The line is thin and the entitlement it carries is a seat and a maintenance, neither of which appears to have been drawn.
**World of upbringing:** Sansvor.
**Licence:** civil flight licence issued on Sansvor, examiner recorded. The examiner's own record lists prior wartime service.
**Service:** contracted. Pension entitlement and dependant citizenship provisions elected in full at enrolment, which is recorded in the file and is not the usual election.

The Restoration notes that thin royal blood is common, that it confers standing and very little money, and that a person holding it who elects the pension provisions in full is telling you what the standing was worth.
