# KING MATTHEW
`INTACT`

The reigning King of the Royal systems, and the man giving the address at the Queen's Anniversary for the fourth year running.

He holds the ordinary powers of the crown, and one that is not ordinary at all. He alone can proclaim an emergency under the High Emergency Council Act, and on that proclamation everything the crown and parliament hold passes to the High Council sitting on Maldo.

The Act says how the powers go across. It does not say for how long, how they come back, or who reviews it. This has been raised in parliament four times and amended none.

It has never been invoked.
