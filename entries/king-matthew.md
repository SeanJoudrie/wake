# KING MATTHEW
`INTACT`

Reigning King. Royal systems.

**Register:** complete.
**Powers:** the crown's ordinary powers, and the sole authority to proclaim an emergency under the High Emergency Council Act, on which proclamation those powers vest in the High Council sitting on Maldo.
**Observances:** has given the address at the Queen's Anniversary in each of the last four years.

The Act has never been invoked. It specifies the proclamation and the vesting and does not specify a term, a review, or a mechanism of return.
