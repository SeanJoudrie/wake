# KINTON
`INTACT`

Fruit, and the geography of who gets it fresh.

Warm, wet, and green all the way round, with forty-one named varieties on the export list and a grading system that Kinton takes far more seriously than anybody buying does.

**The distinction that matters.** Kinton fruit reaches the inner member worlds fresh. It reaches the outer ones as preserve. That is the entire difference between the centre of the galaxy and the edge of it, expressed in whether your fruit is wet or in a jar, and it is visible in the freight schedules and in nothing else anybody would think to look at.

Which is why a piece of fresh Kinton fruit, on a rock in the middle of nowhere, is not really fruit. It is somebody having gone to a great deal of trouble. Guard supply crews carry them out to the far colonies and hand them to children, and those children remember the colour of them for the rest of their lives.
