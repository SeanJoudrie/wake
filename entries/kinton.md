# KINTON
`INTACT`

Agricultural world. Fruit.

**Status:** Guard member state
**Population:** between 20 million and 90 million
**Principal exports:** fresh and preserved fruit

Export returns list forty-one varieties by name and grade. Kinton fruit reaches the outer member worlds as preserve and reaches the inner ones fresh, a distinction visible in the freight schedules and in nothing else.
