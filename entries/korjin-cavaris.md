# KORJIN CAVARIS
`INTACT`

Chemist, engineer, and the man whose name is on the process that fuels the galaxy. Station chief of the extraction works in the Wake field.

**Registration:** GC 4417, a single named patentee, K. Cavaris. Seven steps. Six of them redacted in our copy.
**Assignment:** the licence passes to the Guard Board of Directors. Consideration recorded on the instrument: one credit.
**Contract:** quota. Payment falls due on certification of the full annual quota and not one tonne before.

**Quota performance.** Met, in every year filed. The final year in our holdings shows the quota exceeded, by 443.6 tonnes, which means the certification was days away.

**The station.** Class 2 installation, carrying a quarterly entitlement to point-defence maintenance parts. The entitlement schedules are `INTACT` for every year we hold. The delivery notes are in another series and that series is not complete.

**After.** No death record. No probate. No transfer of the patent to an heir. No subsequent registration anywhere under this name.

A man who invents something of this value generates paper for the rest of his life and for a generation after it. This one generates none, from the assignment onward, and the archive holds no instrument that closes him out.
