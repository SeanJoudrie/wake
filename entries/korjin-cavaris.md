# KORJIN CAVARIS
`INTACT`

Patentee. Named on the registration of the Corefuel synthesis process, GC 4417.

**Registration:** filed GC 4417 under a single named patentee, K. Cavaris. The instrument lists the process in seven steps, six of which are redacted in the copy held here.
**Assignment:** the licence was subsequently assigned to the Guard Board of Directors. The assignment instrument is held. The consideration recorded on it is one credit.
**Operating contract:** a quota contract at the Wake Extraction Station. Payment falling due on certification of the full annual quota and not before.

No further filing has been recovered under this name. No death record, no probate, no transfer of the patent to an heir, and no subsequent registration.

The Restoration notes that a patentee of a process of this value would ordinarily generate a considerable paper trail, and that this one generates none after the assignment.
