# KORNO
`INTACT`

Guard member state. Light engineering and component manufacture.

**Status:** Guard member state
**Population:** between 15 million and 70 million
**Principal exports:** fabricated components, repair services, apprenticeships

The apprentice register is filed and is the world's largest single administrative document. Korno places apprentices with Guard engineering divisions under a standing instrument and has done so since GC 4428.
