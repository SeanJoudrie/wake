# THE KROSTIN SECTOR
`RECOVERED`

A designated volume of space. The designation appears in navigational schedules and is struck through in two of the three copies held here.

`[RECORD DAMAGED]`

The recoverable text of the surviving unmarked copy reads: "[...] transit not advised. See standing notice [...]" The standing notice is not in this archive.

The Restoration has interviewed fourteen navigators. Four declined to discuss the designation. Nine had never encountered it. One asked where we had seen it.
