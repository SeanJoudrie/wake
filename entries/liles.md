# LILES
`INTACT`

Guard Directorate. General officer.

**Commission:** held. Service from a date early in the consolidation period, through general rank, to Directorate.
**Directorship:** the Board seat historically held by a serving or former general officer. The incorporation instrument requires no such seat and the Restoration records the practice and not a rule.
**Interests:** not declared. Directors' commercial interests are not required to be declared and are not.
**Divisions of record:** filed.

Of 1,411 Board resolutions minuted in nineteen years, 1,388 are recorded as carried without a division. There is no provision for recording a dissent and none is recorded against this name or any other.
