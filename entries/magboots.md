# MAGBOOTS
`CONTESTED`

They let you walk up a wall, and almost nobody has a pair.

Sleek, rose-gold, with pointed toes that look far too elegant for what they do. They hum faintly with every step and grip whatever ferrous surface they are set against, which means a man in magboots can climb the outside of a building at a walking pace, clink by clink, and step off the top of it as though he had come up a staircase.

**Why they are rare.** They are pre-war, and the manufacture died with everything else. What is in circulation is what survived. A pair is worth more than most people's contracts, they turn up mostly as loot, and their owners are extremely aware of both facts.

**What they are actually used for.** Not war, mostly. There is a wrecked starship the size of a city on a world nobody names, so thoroughly overgrown that people climb it like a mountain, and the steep sections of it are impossible without a pair. Fathers and sons have gone up it together for generations.

They do not like lightning. A storm will fry a pair permanently, and there is no repairing them, and no replacing them either.
