# MAGBOOTS
`INTACT`

Climbing and hull-work footwear providing controllable magnetic adhesion.

Manufactured before the war and, in a simplified pattern, since. Both patterns are in service. The simplified pattern holds less and is repairable, which most users regard as the better trade.

**Failure.** The control assembly is vulnerable to electrical discharge. Loss to lightning strike and to induced surge appears in the maintenance returns of every world where the equipment is used in the open, and is not repairable at unit level.
