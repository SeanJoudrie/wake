# MALDO
`INTACT`

Working capital of the Royal houses. Seat of parliament, of the ministries, of the embassies, and of the High Council. Formerly an extraction world.

**System:** Maldo (single star, eleven bodies, two habitable)
**Status:** Royal. Administrative capital
**Population:** between 90 million and 400 million
**Membership:** residence and registration. Office is open to any registered resident of five years
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; the Royal court calendar; the old foundry reckoning, retained in guild documents and in the naming of four public holidays
**Principal exports:** administration, arbitration, archival services, antiquities. No primary production

**History.** Five recorded phases. An ordinary habitable world; an extraction boom; a period of heavy industry during which the surface became substantially uninhabitable; exhaustion and abandonment; and rebuilding as the administrative capital.

The rebuilding used the material already present. Every ministry, monument and embassy on Maldo is constructed from Maldo steel produced on Maldo from Maldo ore. The ore is exhausted.

**The city.** Built in a documented order: capital, parliament, monuments, embassies, and then residential sprawl outward from all four. The building registers survive complete for the entire construction period, 61 years, and record 3,308 principal structures.

**The High Council.** Sits here. Its statutory powers are catalogued separately.

**Present character.** Population has declined in each of the last four decennial returns. The registrar's returns record net departure of working-age residents and net arrival of pensioners. The largest single category of registered business on the world is the sale of antiquities and reproductions to visitors.
