# MALDO
`INTACT`

They did not build the capital on a beautiful world. They built it on a used-up one, out of the used-up world itself, and every ministry and monument here is made of the ground it stands on.

**System:** Maldo (single star, eleven bodies, two habitable)
**Status:** Royal. Administrative capital
**Population:** between 90 million and 400 million
**Membership:** residence and registration. Office is open to any registered resident of five years
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; the court calendar; the old foundry reckoning, which survives in guild documents and in the names of four public holidays
**Principal exports:** administration, arbitration, archives, antiques. Nothing is made here now

**Five lives.** An ordinary green world. Then ore, and a boom. Then a century of heavy industry that made the surface uninhabitable and the sky a colour people still describe. Then the ore ran out and everyone left. Then the crown arrived, because a ruin is free land and the material was already lying on site.

**The city.** Built in a documented order, and the building registers survive complete for all sixty-one years of it: capital, then parliament, then monuments, then embassies, then houses spreading outward from all four. 3,308 principal structures.

**The catch.** All of it is Maldo steel, and there is no more Maldo steel. The ore body is worked out. Every repair to a Royal building anywhere is made in some other alloy, and the maintenance you can walk a street and watch it happen, building by building, as less and less of what you are looking at is original.

**Now.** Population has fallen in each of the last four decennial returns. Working-age people leave; pensioners arrive. The largest single category of registered business on the world is the sale of antiques and reproductions to visitors.

The capital of a monarchy is a nice old town that people retire to and take souvenirs from.
