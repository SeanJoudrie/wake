# THE MARFELD BEARD
`TESTIMONY`

The beard grown during the coming-of-age trial and retained afterward. Not an ornament and not a fashion.

**Rule.** The beard begins during the trial, the boy having no means to remove it. On his return it is his. It may be trimmed, shaped, dressed, braided or dyed. It is not shaved.

Shaving is understood on Marfeld as the renunciation of one's family and bloodline. It is not a lesser act than that and there is no intermediate act.

**Meaning.** The local formulation is that hair remembers. What the beard records is the duration of the trial, which is to say how long the man was out there. The Restoration notes that this makes the object a measure and not a symbol, and that it explains why its removal is treated as the destruction of a record rather than a change of appearance.

**Off-world.** Marfeld men in foreign service seek cultural exemption from grooming regulation. Guard exemption applications on this ground are filed. The mean time to determination is 14 months. The grant rate is 71 per cent.

**Note.** The Royal court requires its household and its blood to go clean-shaven, by long ceremonial rule. The two conventions are not compatible and there is no recorded instance of their being reconciled.
