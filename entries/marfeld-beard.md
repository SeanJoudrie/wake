# THE MARFELD BEARD
`TESTIMONY`

On Marfeld they say hair remembers.

The beard starts growing during the trial, because a boy out there has no way to stop it. When he comes back it is his, and what it is is a measurement: how long he was out, written on his face by his own body while it was happening.

You may trim it, shape it, braid it, dye it. Men do all of those and take real care over it.

You do not shave. Shaving is not a haircut on Marfeld and it is not a fashion choice. It is the renunciation of your family and your bloodline, and there is no lesser version of it. There is nothing in between.

Which is why Marfeld men in foreign service spend months arguing with grooming regulations rather than pick up a razor, and why a Marfeld man who is clean-shaven has either done something enormous or is not who he says he is.

**One complication.** The Royal court requires its household and its blood to go clean-shaven, by long ceremonial rule. Nobody has ever reconciled the two.
