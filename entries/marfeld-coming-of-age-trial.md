# THE MARFELD COMING-OF-AGE TRIAL
`TESTIMONY`

At seventeen, a Marfeld boy is put out into the wild with his clothes and nothing else, for weeks.

He is not told beforehand what he is walking into. Not the season, not the country, not which of the three paths he is taking. Blood is the hunt, Root is the forage, Sky is the climb, and which one you get is not yours to choose.

**Where it comes from.** It used to be the first hunting season. You went out with a group, for months, and you came back with meat because the settlement needed the meat. What the modern version keeps is the exposure. What it removed is the group.

**What people will tell you.** Only the parts that happen to other people. Boys who drew winter and crossed frozen rivers on strips of bark. Boys who sewed hides around their feet to keep the toes. Summer, and the insects, and the blisters.

**What nobody will tell you.** Their own. No man who finishes the trial may speak of it, and the rule holds even between men who both went. It is absolute and it is kept.

We asked forty-one Marfeld men about it. All forty-one declined. Two of them apologised.

The original version killed boys. You do not talk about it because some of the ones who walked out with you did not walk back, and the ones who did came home carrying something they had no words for. The silence started as grief and hardened into a rule.
