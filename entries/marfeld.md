# MARFELD
`INTACT`

A world that never lost its big animals. Everywhere else hunted them out. Here they are still in the country, in numbers, and everything about how people live on Marfeld follows from that one fact.

**System:** Marfeld (single star, eight bodies, one habitable)
**Status:** Guard member state
**Population:** between 30 million and 200 million
**Membership:** ritual. There is one route and no exemption, including for people born here
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; the Marfeld reckoning, which has two seasons, hot and cold, and no word for anything between them
**Principal exports:** soldiers, hides, worked bone, draught animals

**Weather.** Two months of heat that kills, two months of cold that kills, and the turn between them comes fast enough that people plan for it the way other worlds plan for a storm. Nothing grows reliably. The diet was a hunting diet for most of the world's history and the cooking still tastes like one.

**Why the towns are the size they are.** A settlement here has to be big enough to wall and small enough to move, because the predators come through on the season and a wall is a delay, not an answer. No town on Marfeld has ever exceeded forty thousand people. No city has ever existed. Nobody local finds this remarkable.

**What they sell.** Marfeld sells men. They appear in the muster rolls of every army in the surviving record, in every period, listed by name and settlement and rate of pay, because Marfeld has kept that book for eleven centuries and keeps it still. They are contracted as professionals and paid as professionals, and the world is quite clear that this is a trade and not a tragedy.

Guard recruiters have had a permanent office here since GC 4409. That is three years after the Guard existed and twenty-two before Marfeld joined it.

**The bombing.** Three times. The first took out the surface of half the northern continent during the war, and no target assessment held by this archive lists Marfeld in that sector at all. The second and third are catalogued as incidental to fighting between other parties.

Casualty figures for all three exist and disagree with one another by a factor of ten.

They rebuilt each time. Their own account of it is not grief and not defiance. It is closer to the way another world would talk about a bad winter.
