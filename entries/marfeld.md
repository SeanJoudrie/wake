# MARFELD
`INTACT`

Continental world of severe axial variation and surviving megafauna. Principal export is soldiers. Bombarded three times in the recorded period, on no occasion as a stated objective.

**System:** Marfeld (single star, eight bodies, one habitable)
**Status:** Guard member state
**Population:** between 30 million and 200 million
**Membership:** ritual. Completion of the coming-of-age trial confers standing. There is no other route recognised locally, including for persons born on the world
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; the Marfeld reckoning, two seasons, hot and cold, with no intermediate terms
**Principal exports:** military personnel, hides, worked bone, domesticated draught megafauna

**Climate.** Approximately two months of extreme heat and two of extreme cold within each cycle, with rapid transition. Agricultural returns show no reliable growing period and a diet historically dependent on hunting.

**Fauna.** The large predators were never eliminated. Settlement is consequently limited in scale: large enough to be walled, small enough to be relocated. No settlement on Marfeld has exceeded 40,000 registered residents in any return we hold, and no urban centre of any kind is recorded in the world's history.

**Export.** Marfeld personnel appear in the muster rolls of every major force in the recovered record, in every period. They are contracted as professionals and are paid at professional rates. The rolls list them by name, settlement, and rate of pay. Guard recruiting has maintained a permanent presence on the world since GC 4409, three years after incorporation, and before the world's own accession.

**Bombardment.** Three events. The first, during the war, destroyed surface infrastructure across approximately half of the northern continent. Guard and Royal target assessments for the relevant sector do not list Marfeld. The second and third are catalogued as incidental to actions between other parties. Casualty figures for all three are estimates and disagree with one another by an order of magnitude.
