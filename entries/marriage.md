# MARRIAGE
`TESTIMONY`

There is no galactic marriage. There is a galactic *filing*, and then there are several hundred entirely unrelated things people actually do.

**The one universal.** On nearly every world, the legal marriage and the real one are different events, and only one of them is witnessed by anybody who matters.

The Guard needs a name on a tax filing. That is the whole of its interest. Everything else, the braid and the run and the pool and the day spent in blindfolds, happens somewhere else, in front of different people, and is the part anybody would tell you about if you asked them when they got married.

On occupied worlds this gap is not an accident. The occupier's ceremony is the one on paper. The other one is done quietly, at home, and is never mentioned to anyone in a uniform.

**What the good ones have in common.** The rites that travel, that survive an occupation, that get carried to a new world by refugees and still work there, all share at least one of four things:

- The marriage is completed by **a physical act rather than a spoken word.** The seventh step. The ninth sip. The last seed. The closed circle. There is a moment everybody present can see, and either it happened or it did not.
- The **contract comes first and outranks the ceremony.** The party is a party.
- The legal and the true marriage are **separate events**, as above.
- There is **a visible exit**, and everybody knows where it is.

**Which direction the money moves** tells you who is scarce. Some worlds pay the bride's family and some pay the groom's, and nobody involved thinks of it as demographics, and it is demographics.

**A note on the war.** Sixty years ago several hard-hit worlds passed emergency measures permitting plural households, on grounds that were entirely about arithmetic. Most were never repealed. They are now a fossil: perfectly legal, rare, mildly embarrassing on some worlds and proudly traditional on others. The Guard has no view, provided one name goes on the filing.

**And a note on citizenship.** Marriage into a member world is one of the two routes to Guard citizenship. The other is enlistment. Which means there is an entire category of marriage in this galaxy that is an immigration instrument, an entire office that audits them, and an unknown number of couples who started as paperwork and did not stay that way.
