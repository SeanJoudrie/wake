# THE MASPIN ALPHABET
`TESTIMONY`

The written system of the Maspin. Flowing, connected, and executed with a quill.

**Character.** Informants of other peoples describe the script as elegant before they describe it as legible. Maspin informants describe it in terms of feeling rather than of sound, and the Restoration has not been able to establish whether the system is alphabetic, syllabic, or something for which we lack a term.

**Use.** Council record, contract, correspondence, and rite. The Council's minute is taken by hand in this script and is not translated.

**Availability.** No Maspin text is held by this archive. Copies of Council submissions to the Royal treasury exist here in Galactic Standard because the Council prepared them in Galactic Standard, addressed to a body that would not otherwise read them.
