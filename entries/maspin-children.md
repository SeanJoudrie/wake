# MASPIN CHILDREN
`TESTIMONY`

Glowing eyes at knee height, and a giggle that visitors describe as wind moving through a hollow tree.

They eat like the adults, which is to say they take food into a body made of smoke and vapour, and the food evaporates into the shimmer as it goes. Watching this happen, more than one human visitor has been left genuinely puzzled about how any of them manages a fork.

They bounce when somebody interesting walks past. They are not shy. And they will tell you things the adults will not, generally without being asked.

> *"You don't find the shaman. The shaman finds you."*
