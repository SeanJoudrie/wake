# MEAD CARDS
`INTACT`

The card game of choice in Guard messes, played in partners, across a table, for nothing.

Two against two. You sit opposite the person you are playing with and you do not get to talk to them about your hand, which is the entire game: everything you want to say has to be said by what you choose to put down. Good partners develop a shorthand over a few hundred rounds. Bad partners develop a grudge.

It travels well because it needs a deck and a flat surface, and a mothership is a place with very few flat surfaces and a great deal of time.

The universal breach of etiquette is narrating your own hand. Everybody knows it is a breach. Everybody does it eventually.
