# MERAM KEST
`CONTESTED`

A Second Sergeant of the Royal Shayron Defence Force, under a name that is not the one he was given.

The name is issued rather than inherited. It is what the Crown puts on his papers when he is working somewhere the Crown would rather not be seen working, and it is a real name in every sense that matters to a customs officer and no sense at all that matters to his mother.

He recites the oath the way soldiers do, front to back, without needing to find the start of it:

> *"I serve the people of Shayron and uphold the dignity of the"*

**What he is like.** A man who very badly wants there to be a procedure. He asks for the code phrase. He wants to know which part of this is the official part, because if he can find that, then the rest of it is something he can be told what to do about.

He also has somebody he answers to whose name he was not supposed to say.
