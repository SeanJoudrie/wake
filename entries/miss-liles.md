# MISS LILES
`INTACT`

Secretary to a Director, and the other name on a guardianship registered twenty years ago.

**Employment:** civilian establishment, Directorate. Grade and length of service entered
**Present:** absent on medical grounds. The condition is entered and is gastric

**The guardianship.** Twenty years back, in the allowances schedule, a temporary guardianship in two names. Hers is the second. She was in her middle twenties and not yet married to the first.

The allowance runs, and then it is discontinued at the claimant's request, the child having passed to Academy wardship. The claimant named on the discontinuation is her husband.

Hers is not on it.

**A note on the establishment.** A Director's secretary sees the correspondence, the diary, and everyone who comes through the door. The Guard's records schedule classifies the post as civilian clerical and requires no vetting for it beyond the standard.

The series ends here.
