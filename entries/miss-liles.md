# MISS LILES
`INTACT`

The Director's wife, and his secretary, which means she runs the diary, the correspondence and the door.

Twenty years ago she was his girlfriend, they were both barely out of college, and they took in an eight-year-old who had just lost everyone. She fed him, put a cake in front of him on his birthday, and was unfailingly kind to a boy who spent the next two decades quietly pulling away from her.

She is currently off sick with ulcers, and somebody else is covering her desk. A pilot, as it happens, who is very good at diaries and did not ask for the job.
