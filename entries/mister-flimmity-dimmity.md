# MISTER FLIMMITY DIMMITY
`TESTIMONY`

A floom. He belongs, in the loosest possible sense, to Calsco Fritt.

His collar tag reads:

> *Mister Flimmity Dimmity. If lost, return to Calsco Fritt.*

He gets lost. When he does, a seven-foot Katanzarian goes through the undergrowth singing *Flimmity Dimmity, where are you, Flimmity Dimmity, I miss you*, in a voice one witness described as belonging to somebody who had gargled gravel.

**He is also a judge of character**, which is what flooms are. They make up their minds about a person quickly and people on Maspin act on it. Flimmity's opinion of a stranger has changed at least one important decision that this archive knows about, and that stranger has no idea.
