# THE MONVER REFUSAL
`TESTIMONY`

The founding event of Monver. A colonial force refused an order to exterminate the population it had been sent to displace, and fought its own fleet.

**Date.** Approximately two thousand years before the present. No document survives from either party. The sending government has not existed for a comparable period and is not identified in any account we hold.

**Account.** Consistent across every Monveri source and not attested anywhere else. A colonial expedition established itself over decades, mingled with the native population, and received an order for their extermination. The expedition refused. The refusal became a guerrilla war against the fleet that had brought it, fought on the ground the expedition had settled, and won.

**Inheritance.** Every distinctive feature of Monveri political life is stated locally as descending from this event: the primacy of local government over distant authority, the refusal to impose on any other world's independence, and the fact that Monver has never founded a colony.

**Note.** Monver's accession instrument with the Guard provides two years of universal service in exchange for guarantees of non-interference in local government, statute and observance. Monveri sources describe the instrument as the same bargain their founders were given, drafted this time by the party that has to keep it.

Attributed to eleven informants across four settlements.
