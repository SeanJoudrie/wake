# MONVER
`INTACT`

Forested mountain world. Guard member state on negotiated terms. Supplies personnel to the Guard at a rate substantially above its share of population.

**System:** Monver (single star, five bodies, one habitable, one exploited)
**Status:** Guard member state. Acceded GC 4431 on negotiated terms
**Population:** between 80 million and 350 million
**Membership:** two years of service and non-interference. Naturalisation is granted on completion of the service obligation. There is no other route and no exemption
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; the Monver local reckoning, six seasons, anchored to the high thaw
**Principal exports:** personnel, timber, engineering trades, regulated recreational botanicals

**Terrain.** Mountains, forest, high beaches, and interior dry country. Several of the ranges are, on survey, the overgrown hulls of colonial-era vessels. The largest is 31 kilometres on its long axis and carries mature forest.

**Accession terms.** Monver's instrument is the most heavily qualified in the member register. It reserves local government, local statute on recreational botanicals, and all local observances, in each case by name. It provides two years of universal service. The service is delivered in trade instruction as much as in arms, and the trade certificates issued under it are recognised across the member worlds.

**Contribution.** Monver holds approximately 0.9 per cent of member population and provides 4.1 per cent of member personnel. It has done so in every year since accession.

**Planetary Defence Force.** Retained under the accession terms and not dissolved. Monveri units served on both sides of the conflict at [...] before that force was merged into the member structure. `[RECORD DAMAGED]`

**Note.** Health returns record a persistent minority of long-term users of local botanicals for whom the effect is not recreational. The returns are filed annually. No local statute addresses them and no local authority has proposed one.
