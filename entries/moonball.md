# MOONBALL
`INTACT`

The game they play on the ice. Modanick's national sport and the thing that world will actually raise its voice about.

Eleven a side, a stick, a weighted disc, and a frozen canal or a prepared rink. The season starts when the water goes over, which is the second week of winter most years, and everyone plays: children on the canals, works teams, a league with a published rulebook older than the war.

The Modanick championship is the best-attended thing that happens on that world all year, and it is not close. The canals fill with people who cannot get tickets and watch nothing in particular together.

Variants are played on forty-four worlds, and every mothership has a court in the rec room with a floor waxed to a shine and an argument in progress on it. Modanick's is the only code anyone has ever bothered to write down properly, and Modanick will tell you so.
