# NANCAST
`TESTIMONY`

World of the interior. Known outside its own system for one export.

**Population:** between 5 million and 40 million
**Principal exports:** cast iron figurines

The figurines are small, standing, and faceless. Informants describe them as household objects rather than devotional ones, and are consistent that the absence of a face is not a stylistic economy but the point of the object. None of our informants was able to say what the practice is for, and two were surprised to be asked.

Attributed to V., Nancast, and to H., Maldo.
