# THE NHATH
`CONTESTED`

There are two Nhath in the surviving record and they are not the same people.

**The one in the invoices.** Guard contracting files, `INTACT`, complete in the way commercial files always are: scope, duration, rate, settlement. Eleven separate engagements across nineteen years. Paid in full and on the scheduled date, every time.

The fund that paid them is identified by an internal reference. We have not established what it denotes. `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`

Guard ministerial material describes the same people as raiders without political organisation.

**The one in the grammar.** Recovered language material, and it describes something else entirely. Verbs that conjugate by subject. Two levels of *you*, chosen by the status of the person you are addressing, where the wrong choice is not a rudeness. Numbers in base eight, named after the fingers, extending without limit. Colours that are all derived from things: red is blood-colour, white is clean-colour, silver is weapon-colour.

And a genealogy. Every Nhath carries a generation number, counted in eights, inherited and incremented. The recovered examples run into three figures.

A people with no political organisation does not keep an eighty-eight generation line. A people that keeps one is not described by an invoice.

**How they say themselves.** Their word for themselves is their word for *we*. Everyone else in the galaxy calls them by it, which means every world in this archive addresses them, without knowing it, as *us*.

There is one word for everybody outside: *krayd*. It serves for *they*, for *other species*, and for *enemy*. The language provides nothing neutral for a stranger.

**Work.** Armed contract, which is what the Guard bought and therefore what the record shows. Also haulage, salvage, and the sale of water rights. The survey notes an instructor of arithmetic at a settlement school, by name and generation number, and records his rate of pay.
