# THE NHATH
`CONTESTED`

Non-human people of a dry world. Present in the record in two forms which do not describe the same population.

**The commercial record.** `INTACT`. Nhath parties appear in Guard contracting files as engaged personnel. The files are complete in the way commercial files are: scope, duration, rate, and settlement. Payment is recorded as made in full and on the scheduled date in every instance held here, across eleven separate engagements over nineteen years. The paying fund is identified in the files by an internal reference. The Restoration has not been able to establish what that reference denotes. `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`

Guard ministerial material describes the same people as raiders without political organisation.

**The linguistic record.** `RECOVERED` and `TESTIMONY`. The surviving language material describes a population with systematic verb conjugation, two registers of address distinguished by status, a base-eight numeral system extending to compound prefixes, a derived colour system with no abstract colour terms, and a genealogical convention in which each individual carries a numbered generation count. Counts in the recovered material run into the hundreds.

The two records cannot both be a complete account. A people without political organisation does not keep an eighty-eight generation lineage, and a people that keeps one is not adequately described by an invoice.

**Economy.** Contract work of several kinds, of which armed work is the best documented because armed work is what the Guard bought. Also recorded: haulage, salvage, and the sale of water rights. The surviving survey material notes an instructor of arithmetic attached to a settlement school, by name and generation number, and records his rate of pay.

**Nomenclature.** The people's name for themselves is their first person plural. Every other population addresses them by their word for us.
