# OPERATION THORN CROWN
`INTACT`

A Guard Central Intelligence Branch operational brief, classified eyes only, held here in full.

**[SPOILER] What it is.** A plan to end Shayron's sovereign independence without invading it. The stated objective is a Guard-aligned proxy state, achieved by installing a compliant monarch. The brief is explicit that the point is to keep the appearance of traditional government while holding royal law, economic policy and military deployment.

**[SPOILER] What it rests on.** Queen Traceen has been absent for four years with no credible sighting. She is presumed dead or compromised. The public account of her absence remains ambiguous, which the brief treats as an asset rather than a problem.

King Matthew is assessed as politically ineffectual and dependent on the High Council. The brief names the three levers to be used on him: emotional fatigue, legacy insecurity, and appeals to stability.

The High Council is assessed as fragmented, with several members open to leverage or bribery and at least three confirmed as Guard assets or handlers.

**[SPOILER] The cells.** Three deep cover cells, designated Ares-9, Lira-2 and Castor-6, have been embedded in royal sectors for years. Their work to date is listed as covert communications and surveillance, the compromising of administrative clerks and security liaisons, and shifts in public opinion produced through controlled media agents.

**[SPOILER] The phases.** Escalate conflict within the Council and exploit the succession. Induce an emergency by staging a catastrophic event, named in the brief as an economic crash, a security breach, or a false-flag attack, in order to force the King to surrender executive power under emergency protocols. Engineer a Council majority handing that authority to Guard-controlled councillors. Crown a vetted monarch under Guard advisory.

**[SPOILER] The timing.** The false flag is scheduled for the anniversary of Queen Traceen's Ascension, a national holiday with mass public participation, royal ceremony and ceremonial units on the streets.

The brief gives three reasons for that choice and does not hide any of them. Crowd density guarantees maximum psychological trauma. Collapsing the state on the day that honours her reign reframes her absence as divine approval for a new order. The honour guards, ceremonial units and state media provide cover for pre-positioning assets.

The planned cover story is a coordinated assault by radical separatists from the outer provinces, framed as an anti-monarchist uprising. The listed targets include the Central Palace grounds and the royal procession route.

**[SPOILER] Who signed it.** Authorised by Colonel Vesk Iradin of the Strategic Covert Ops Division. Distribution is Tier 1, operative eyes only. The recommendation is to advance to Phase II pending final clearance from Directorate Liles, with all field cells holding operational silence until a coded transmission from Command Node Sable-3.

**[SPOILER] How it came to be read.** Two enlisted men opened it on a terminal they had no authority to open. Neither of them was meant to see it and the operation does not account for them.
