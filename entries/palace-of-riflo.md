# THE PALACE OF RIFLO
`TESTIMONY`

The big stone building at the heart of Riflo, holding the Council chamber and the temple attached to it.

Vines have been growing into the stonework for a very long time and were never cleared, so the building and the jungle have arrived at an arrangement. Visitors consistently describe it as older than the settlement around it. No foundation date exists and no local authority claims to have one.

The temple is kept by a caretaker staff, some of whom are working off an indenture, which on Maspin is lawful and is administered by the Council that sits upstairs.
