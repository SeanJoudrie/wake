# THE PALACE OF RIFLO
`TESTIMONY`

Principal civic structure of Riflo and seat of the Council. Contains the Council Chamber and the temple establishment attached to it.

Stone construction with substantial vine growth incorporated rather than cleared. Informants describe the building as older than the settlement around it. No foundation date is recorded and no local authority claims to hold one.

The temple establishment is maintained by a caretaker staff. A proportion of that staff serve under indenture, which is lawful on the world and is catalogued separately.
