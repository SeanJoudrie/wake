# THE PATH OF BLOOD
`TESTIMONY`

One of the three paths of the Marfeld trial. The hunt.

The boy is put out into predator country and feeds himself by taking animals. What he is given to face is not disclosed to him beforehand and is understood to vary by season and by ground.

Marfeld megafauna are large enough that a settlement cannot hold ground against them year-round. A boy alone is not expected to take one. What he is expected to do is not stated in any account available to us.
