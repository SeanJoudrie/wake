# THE PATH OF BLOOD
`TESTIMONY`

The hunt. You are put out into predator country and you feed yourself off what you can kill.

Marfeld's big animals are big enough that a walled settlement cannot hold ground against them all year. A boy on his own with a sharpened stick is not expected to take one of those. What he is expected to do instead is something nobody outside will explain, and everyone inside already knows.

Of the three paths this is the one outsiders assume is hardest. On Marfeld opinion is divided, and the division is usually along the lines of which one the person you are asking walked.
