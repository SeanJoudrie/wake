# THE PATH OF ROOT
`TESTIMONY`

One of the three paths of the Marfeld trial. The forage.

The boy feeds himself from the ground. On a world with no reliable growing period and two extreme seasons, this is the path that requires the most prior knowledge and the least equipment.

It is described by informants from other worlds as the easy path. It is not described that way on Marfeld.
