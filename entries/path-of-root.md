# THE PATH OF ROOT
`TESTIMONY`

The forage. You feed yourself off the ground.

On a world with no reliable growing season and two killing extremes, this is the path that needs the most knowledge and the least equipment. You have to already know what comes up where, in which month, and what will put you on your back. Nobody teaches it formally. You learned it or you did not.

Visitors call it the easy one. Nobody on Marfeld does.
