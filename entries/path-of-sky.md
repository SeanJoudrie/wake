# THE PATH OF SKY
`TESTIMONY`

One of the three paths of the Marfeld trial. The climb.

The boy ranges vertically. The relevant country is a formation of sheer pillar mountains, near-columnar, rising from the plain in isolation. He feeds himself by taking eggs and honey from nests and hives sited on the faces, against the objection of what built them.

Flying fauna of substantial size are recorded in the same country.

It is the only one of the three paths from which there is a view, which is understood on Marfeld to be why a boy would choose it, and which is understood to be the reason a boy is not permitted to choose.
