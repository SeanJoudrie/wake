# THE PATH OF SKY
`TESTIMONY`

The climb. Straight up.

The country for this is a formation of pillar mountains, near-vertical columns standing alone off the plain, and you go up them. You eat by taking eggs and honey out of nests and hives on the faces, while the things that built them object at close range. Some of those things are large and most of them can fly.

It is the only one of the three with a view at the end, which is exactly why a boy would pick it, and exactly why a boy is not allowed to pick.
