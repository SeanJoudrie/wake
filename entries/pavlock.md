# PAVLOCK
`INTACT`

Marfeld national in Guard service.

**Muster roll:** held. Marfeld files its exported personnel by name, settlement, and rate of pay, and has done so for eleven centuries. The entry is complete.
**Settlement of origin:** recorded.
**Trial:** completion recorded. Marfeld registers the fact of completion and never the content.
**Exemption:** an application for cultural exemption from Guard grooming regulation appears in the exemptions file, granted, after fourteen months.

The Restoration observes that this is a fuller record of a serving soldier than the Guard itself will release, and that it exists because a small world on the rim keeps better books about its people than the institution employing them.
