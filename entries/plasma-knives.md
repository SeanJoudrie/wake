# PLASMA KNIVES
`INTACT`

A blade that is not a blade, carried by everybody who cannot afford to run out of options.

It lives in the forearm gauntlet. A press of the panel and it snaps into being with a hard hiss: a dense, crackling wedge of blue-teal light, throwing its glow across the wearer's armour and the face of whoever is about to have a bad few seconds.

**It does not last.** A few moments, and then it is gone and the gauntlet needs to cool. That is the whole design constraint, and everything about how it is used follows from it. You do not fence with a plasma knife. You solve one problem with it and then you do not have it any more.

It goes through flesh like the flesh has agreed to it, and it will grind to a stop against bone. It cuts a clean glowing hole through a locked drawer. It cauterises as it goes, which is either mercy or the opposite depending on where it lands.

**The bayonet variant** mounts under a rifle barrel and burns longer, at the cost of being attached to something two feet long.

**The shield variant** is the same principle turned flat: a small teal plane of energy that does not block so much as burn. Pressed against something, it does not stop it. It cooks it.
