# THE POLARIS SYSTEM
*also known as: the breadbasket*
`INTACT`

Ten inhabited bodies under a single agricultural economy and a single calendar. Supplies the greater part of the staple food of the member worlds.

**Composition:** two principal planets, Kelton and Weton, and eight worked moons.
**Status:** Guard administration is seated on Kelton. The outer bodies retain local government. Licensing, banking and the harvest ledger are held on Kelton
**Population:** between 200 million and 900 million across all ten bodies
**Membership:** application. Agricultural settlement is by application and is granted on assessment. Applications exceed places in every year returned
**Calendars in use:** the Polaris Harvest Reckoning, shared across all ten bodies, because the logistics require it. Galactic Calendar for external contracts only
**Principal exports:** grain, flour, oil, protein cake, meat, salt, seed stock, alcohol

**Structure.** The system is a single machine with the parts on separate worlds. Weton grows grain. Dorrow mills it. Sheel produces meat and, from the same operations, the bone, blood and manure on which Weton's soil depends. Corr supplies water. Schriggé supplies salt. Tspa supplies pulse protein. Tall holds the seed lines. Baline holds the cold and the freight. Kelton holds the paperwork.

**Terms of residence.** Guaranteed housing, guaranteed purchase of crop at a scheduled price, wage floors, a weekly rest day, and a maintained repair entitlement for agricultural machinery. The Restoration has examined comparable terms across forty-one agricultural worlds and finds none better.

Departure from the system requires an approved leave filing stating a date of return. The requirement appears in the standard residence instrument at clause 11 and is enforced by the port authority on Kelton. Filings are recorded as approved in 96.8 per cent of applications.

**Currency.** Polaris scrip. Catalogued separately. It is not negotiable outside the system.

**Libraries.** The system's public libraries are funded from the harvest levy and are unusually well stocked, holding a mean of 41,000 volumes per settlement against a member-world mean of 9,200. The acquisitions schedules are filed. Political and constitutional works do not appear in them.
