# PORTABOARDS
`CONTESTED`

One of the last mass-produced Levitation Matrix devices, and nobody alive knows how to make another.

It floats. That is the short answer and the one most people give. It carries a rider or a load a few feet off the ground, glides over broken country and standing water, and wobbles when the field has to adjust to uneven terrain, which is often.

The long answer is that the Levitation Matrix was a whole class of anti-gravity engineering that came out of the pre-war world and did not survive it. What is left in circulation is what was already built. They are traded, hoarded, stolen, and repaired by people who do not understand the thing they are repairing.

**Why an engineer will trade everything he owns for one.** Not to ride it. To open it. Take one apart, work out what the matrix is actually doing, and a great deal of what the galaxy currently cannot do becomes possible again.

Several people have tried. So far the boards have survived the attempts better than the reputations of the people making them.
