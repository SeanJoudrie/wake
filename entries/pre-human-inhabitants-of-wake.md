# PRE-HUMAN INHABITANTS OF WAKE
`TESTIMONY`

Something was on Wake before us, and it is buried in the mantle.

That is legend, and we would not print it at all except for one thing: it is told, in recognisable form, by at least six populations that had no contact with each other before the war. Traditions that share nothing else share this.

**Where the versions agree.** That they were very old. That they reached a point where they no longer died. That this turned out not to be what they wanted. That they had a hand in making humans afterwards. And that something of theirs is still in us, described in every version as a fault rather than a gift.

**Where they disagree.** Everything else, including whether any of them is left.

There is no artefact, no structure, no remains and no document. Ruins built by somebody nobody can identify do exist, on Katanzaria and elsewhere, which proves there were predecessors somewhere and proves nothing at all about these.
