# PRE-HUMAN INHABITANTS OF WAKE
`TESTIMONY`

A people held by several unconnected traditions to have inhabited Wake before humans, and to be interred beneath its mantle.

**Status of the account.** Legend. It is carried in the oral traditions of at least six populations that had no contact with one another before the war, which is the only reason this archive records it at all. No artefact, no structure, no remains and no document supports it.

**Content, where the traditions agree.** That they were of great age. That they achieved a condition in which they did not die. That the condition was not what they expected. That they made or intervened in the making of humans afterward, and that something in them persists in humans as a fault rather than a gift.

**Content, where the traditions disagree.** Everything else, including whether any of them remains.

The Restoration notes that ruins of a construction no recorded people can reproduce do exist, on Katanzaria and elsewhere, and that this establishes the existence of predecessors somewhere and nothing at all about these.
