# PRE-WAR LANGUAGE FAMILIES
`INFERRED`

Before the war, several large human cultures held territory across multiple worlds, each with its own language. Some had done so for a thousand years or more. They fought one another periodically without any of them being destroyed.

They ended between roughly four hundred and one hundred years ago. The cause in each case was the collapse of movement: supply chains stopped, trade stopped, and worlds ceased to be able to reach one another. No multi-world human polity survived the war. What survived were planets, some of which acceded to the Guard and some of which did not.

The languages continued to be spoken locally. They ceased to be spoken between worlds.

**Evidence.** No literature, speech recordings or grammars are held here. The families are reconstructed from suffixes on proper names, which appear in shipping and licensing registers across worlds that have no relationship to one another.

**Kanta.** The language of the human homeworld and the oldest human tongue attested here. It marked descent. A name ending in *-zin*, *-zon*, *-jin* or *-in* denoted the son of a named person.

The ending is still functional in some lines. *Ninzin*, *Jayzin* and *Iradin* are inherited family names. In other cases it has become a given name with no patronymic force. *Jimzon* is a given name meaning the son of Jim. His father's given name was *Korjin*, meaning the son of Kor.

Kanta has the widest distribution of the three families named here. Its home planet was destroyed and it has no remaining first-language speakers.

**Myatugo.** Marked a holding: ground registered to a named party. The suffix is *-ton* or *-tom*.

It appears on **Kelton**, **Weton** and **Kinton**. Kelton and Weton are the two planets of the Polaris system. Kinton is four systems distant with no recorded connection to either. All three are agricultural worlds.

**Saolai.** The clerical and administrative language, used in law, accounting, scholarship and court ceremonial. The suffix is a final *-o*, *-a* or *-ia*.

It appears on **Maldo**, **Creta**, **Katanzaria**, **Kitalia**, **Saigo**, **Korno** and **Riflo**. Maldo is the Royal administrative capital. Creta is the principal banking world. Katanzaria maintains a civil registry running eleven centuries. Saigo is the chartered university world.

**Other families.** At least four further suffix groups are attested and some had wider distribution than the three above. They appear as *-ar*, *-or* and *-vor*; as *-eld*; as *-ow*; and on two worlds whose registered names carry a diacritic that Galactic Standard does not use and that roughly half of all filings omit.

These families are not named here. Identification would require material produced in the languages themselves, and no such material has been recovered.

**Convergence of two suffixes.** Myatugo *-ton* and Kanta *-zon* are unrelated in origin and have converged in form. **Penston**, **Armstin** and **Krostin** are consistent with either derivation and cannot be assigned. The homeworld's older name, **Waketin**, is consistent with both simultaneously.

**Irregularities.** *Penston* is a personal name carrying a Myatugo suffix. *Astrovin* is a commodity carrying a Kanta suffix. Cross-borrowing of this kind is normal between languages in contact and is not treated here as evidence against the reconstruction.
