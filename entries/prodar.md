# PRODAR
`CONTESTED`

A name, and nothing attached to it.

He is spoken of once, by a man who trusted him, in a sentence that came out faster than the man intended and stopped him cold halfway through. He was not asked about and he was not explained. The name simply arrives, carrying the weight of somebody with the authority to be certain about, and then it goes.

No rank. No world. No service. No description of a face, a voice, or a room he has ever been in.

Whoever he is, at least one soldier of the Royal Shayron Defence Force believes Prodar knows exactly where he is and approves of it.
