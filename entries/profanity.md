# PROFANITY
`INTACT`

Four words do almost all of the work, they are the same four words on every world in this volume, and they are older than anything else still in general use.

**What is actually said.** Two of them carry the load. One is a verb doing duty as a verb, a noun, an adjective, an intensifier and a piece of punctuation, and in the recorded material it appears in that last role more often than in all the others together. The second is a noun for waste that has quietly become the word for any situation. After those, a distant third for the underworld and a distant fourth for damnation, both of which survive in mouths that have never held the belief either one came from.

Everything else is regional, weak, and disappearing.

**The thing worth noticing.** Nobody teaches this. It is not in the centrally produced material, has never been printed in a primer, and no foundation anywhere has ever taught it under contract.

It is also, of everything in Galactic Standard, the part that varies least. A Guardsman off Modanick and a dock hand on a world that has never had a garrison reach for the same word, in the same position in the sentence, at the same provocation.

Nobody transmitted it deliberately. It moved anyway, on ships, in messes, between strangers with nothing else in common, and it arrived intact.

**Where it is not allowed.** Two of the eleven tower proprietors refuse it outright, which is why there are worlds where a broadcast drama sounds like nobody has ever been annoyed.

**A separate class entirely.** The words this volume catalogues as `Crag` and `Scarg` are not profanity and are not treated as profanity by anybody. They are ordinary vocabulary that happens to do harm. Nobody lowers their voice for either, no proprietor refuses either, and no notice anywhere discourages the second one at all.

Which is the whole distinction. The words people apologise for are the ones that mean nothing.
