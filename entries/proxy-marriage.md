# PROXY MARRIAGE
`INTACT`

There is a form. Of course there is a form.

**Why it exists.** The Guard runs a rotation that can put a person six months from anywhere, and it does not adjust that rotation for weddings. So the galaxy long ago accepted that one half of a marriage may not be in the room, and built the machinery to handle it.

**How the absent party appears.** As a projection, where the relay will hold, which it frequently will not. As a stand-in, usually a sibling or a squadmate, who says the words and is then teased about it for the rest of their life. Or not at all: an empty frame set where a person should be standing, and everybody proceeds around it.

**The empty frame is the older custom** and the one people find harder to watch.

**The paperwork.** Countersigned, witnessed at both ends, filed against a service number. It is entirely routine. There is a queue for it. The clerk who processes them has processed thousands and can do one in under four minutes.

**And the version nobody puts on a poster.** Some worlds permit a marriage to be registered after one party has died, on application, with authorisation from an office high enough that the applications are counted in single figures per year. The reasons given are pensions, legitimacy and inheritance. The reasons given are not the reasons.
