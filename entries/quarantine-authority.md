# THE QUARANTINE AUTHORITY
`INTACT`

Biosecurity authority of the Polaris system. Seated in Kelton City. Regulates movement of organic material between the ten inhabited bodies.

**Basis.** The system grows single crops at planetary scale. A blight established on Weton would not be contained on Weton. The authority exists to prevent that and has, on the record, prevented it: no system-wide crop failure appears in any return this archive holds.

**Powers.** Inspection, seizure, destruction, and the closure of movement between bodies. Closure requires no external approval and is not appealable. Movement of persons is subject to the same instrument as movement of material.

**Use.** Filed. Closures ordered GC 4442 to GC 4461: 61. Mean duration 9 days. Longest, 214 days, Dorrow to Kelton, in a year in which the Dorrow milling wage was in dispute.

The authority's returns record the biological grounds for each closure. The Restoration has verified the grounds in every case it could check and could check 44 of the 61.
