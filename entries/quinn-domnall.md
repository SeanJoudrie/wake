# QUINN DOMNALL
`INTACT`

An accountant. Not a soldier, and about to be sent somewhere that will not care about the difference.

Deep lines cut into his face and heavy bags under his eyes, and neither of them arrived in a hurry. He looks tired in a way that has stopped being a state and become a feature.

**The record, in his own words.** Basic at Fort Actom. 704B, Accountant. Advanced training on Maldo. Officer school at Wedear. Led supply routes out to Fawn. He can recite it in about eight seconds and does, whenever somebody implies he has never been anywhere.

He works in the accounting division on the Mothership, under fluorescent light that hums, retyping twenty years of financial records by hand because the war killed every computer and Guardnet is barely walking. It is enormous, pointless, and never finished.

Years ago he started putting small deliberate errors into the ledgers. A private rebellion, one number at a time. Nobody has ever noticed, because what the division wanted from twenty years of retyping was the twenty years and not the numbers.

Older than the men he is about to be standing next to. Precise. Not brave and not a coward. A man who worked out a long time ago that his work does not matter and kept doing it correctly anyway.

His contract is nearly up.

**And then a file lands on his desk.** A transfer, to a security posting on a frozen moon called Sonaff. He is being sent to guard a hangar.
