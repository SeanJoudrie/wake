# QUINN DOMNALL
`INTACT`

Guard accounting division. One of the five named on the Sonaff assignment order.

**Division:** accounting. Engaged on the reconstruction of the pre-Wipe ledger, a manual programme running from GC 4402.
**Contract:** term recorded, with an expiry date.
**Transfer:** static security, sealed installation, Sonaff. The transfer order is dated within the final year of his contract.

The Restoration holds no explanation for the transfer of a clerical grade to a security posting, and the establishment schedules for the installation do not list a clerical requirement.
