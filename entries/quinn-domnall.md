# QUINN DOMNALL
`INTACT`

Accounting. Older than the men he ends up standing next to. One of the five named on the Sonaff assignment order.

**Division:** accounting, Mothership. Engaged on the manual reconstruction of the pre-Wipe ledger
**Contract:** term entered, with an expiry date, and the expiry date is close
**Transfer:** static security, sealed installation, Sonaff. The transfer order falls inside the final year of his contract
**Classification:** clerical. It is not amended by the transfer

**What we noticed.** We are clerks. We went through the reconstructed ledger pages attributed to this hand because they were available, and the error rate is wrong.

It is not high. It is stable. Across twenty years of retyping it does not fall the way a copyist's rate falls with practice, and the errors do not cluster where fatigue puts them. They are distributed evenly, in small amounts, in fields that reconcile against nothing.

We are not in a position to say what that means. We are in a position to say that no audit has ever remarked on it, because no audit has ever been performed, because what the division wanted from twenty years of retyping was the twenty years and not the numbers.

**The posting.** The establishment schedule for that installation lists no clerical requirement. We hold no explanation for sending a man who counts to guard a door.

The series ends here.
