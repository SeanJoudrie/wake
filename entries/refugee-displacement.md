# THE REFUGEE DISPLACEMENT
`INFERRED`

The largest movement of people that has ever happened, and the thinnest file in this archive.

Somewhere between four and nine billion, by our own estimate, from housing stock, ration issues and port arrivals on forty-one worlds. Other figures in circulation run from two billion to eleven.

**Why it broke everything.** The receiving worlds could not manufacture. Housing, water, medicine and food could be moved and could not be made. A world of four hundred million taking in a billion people was not facing a policy question. It was facing arithmetic.

**What worlds did, from the port records:**

- Took everyone, 9 worlds
- Took them and camped them, 22 worlds
- Took some, chosen against stated criteria, 31 worlds
- Refused, 19 worlds
- Refused, and fired on the ships, 6 worlds

Each of those is a category of surviving document, because port authorities file their orders and the orders lasted.

**What it did to the map.** People moved along survival lines, not along which side they were on. So almost every world came out of it holding a population from the other side of the war, and the fighting inside borders started before anybody declared anything.

**And what everyone learned.** That where you were standing decided whether you lived. Which is why, when the Guard offered free movement between member worlds, that is the clause the accession debates argue about.

**Still unfinished.** Whether the descendants of those arrivals are citizens is a live question in the statute of forty-four worlds in this volume. In twelve of them it is unresolved in the third generation.
