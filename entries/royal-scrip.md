# ROYAL SCRIP
`INTACT`

The crown's own money. Struck and issued from Maldo, accepted in the Royal systems and in the protectorates, and it is the instrument in which every tax, due and royalty the houses collect is assessed.

**What it is worth.** What the houses say. There is no metal behind it, no grain, no reserve of anything, and no schedule anywhere setting out what would have to happen for that to change. It is worth something because the Royal treasury will take it back, and the Royal treasury will take it back because it issued it.

**The name.** *Scrip* is not the word the ministries use. The ministries say the Royal credit, in every instrument, without exception. Everybody else on every dock in the galaxy says scrip, and has since before anybody now living was working, and the two words have never once appeared in the same document.

Which is the entire political situation in one noun. Polaris pays its farmers in a currency that is excellent and only good in Polaris, and every person in that system knows exactly what that arrangement is. The crown does the same thing across two systems and a string of protectorates, and the difference is that on Maldo it is not sayable.

**Against the credit.** Not published. There is a rate, it moves, and it is quoted between banks and by nobody in public. The treasury has never printed one and has never been asked in a form it would have to answer.

The Restoration has looked for a stated conversion in nineteen years of ministerial filing and has not found one. What we have found, repeatedly, is contracts written in both units on the same page with no bridge between them, which is what people do when the answer is understood and nobody wants it in writing.

**What comes in.** Taxes from the Royal systems. Dues from the protectorates. Royalties from arrangements with third parties, of which by far the largest is Astonia, which pays by the head.

**And the problem with that.** The Astonian accounting is performed by the Guard. The treasury receives a figure and a payment in its own money, and does not receive the returns underneath, and has not, on the record, asked for them.

So the crown's single largest revenue line is a number somebody else worked out, converted at a rate nobody publishes, into a currency the crown values itself.

**What it will not buy.** Nothing scheduled. Nothing unreplaceable. Maldo steel is worked out and no quantity of Royal scrip will produce another tonne of it, which the maintenance books on every Royal building record in the plainest possible way, one repair at a time.

*[Compiler's note: the treasury on Maldo has never failed to honour this money and we should say so. It has also never been asked to honour very much of it at once.]*
