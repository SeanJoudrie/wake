# ROYAL SHAYRON DEFENCE FORCE
`INTACT`

The Crown's own soldiers, and the other army in the room whenever the Guard is in it.

Shayron keeps a real military. Not the ivory line on the approach to the castle, which is ceremony, but formations with ranks, postings, transports and a rank structure that runs its own way: first and second sergeants, a whole ladder of them, arranged to a logic that predates the Guard and has never been brought into line with it.

**How close they are to the Guard.** Close. They train together, exercise together, and lend each other people, in the ordinary manner of two militaries that have never fought each other and both benefit from the arrangement. A Royal officer on a Guard transport is unusual enough to be worth a second look and not unusual enough to be reported.

The oath is recited rather than sworn, and every soldier in the force has it by heart:

> *"I serve the people of Shayron and uphold the dignity of the"*

**Names.** A Royal soldier working outside Shayron very often is not using the one on his file. This is normal, it is authorised, and everybody on both sides of the arrangement knows it, which does not stop it being awkward whenever somebody says it out loud.
