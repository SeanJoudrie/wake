# SAIGO
`INTACT`

Warm coastal world. Seat of the Guard university and the principal engineering centre of the member worlds. Site of the unmanned systems reconstruction programme.

**System:** Saigo (single star, six bodies, one habitable)
**Status:** Guard member state and chartered university world
**Population:** between 50 million and 250 million
**Membership:** idea. Residence and rate. Matriculation confers residence
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar; the university term calendar, which governs everything on the world including its shipping
**Principal exports:** engineers, patents, instruments, computational research

**The programme.** Every drone, remote and unmanned system in the galaxy ceased to function in GC 4401 and none was recoverable. The reconstruction programme was chartered at Saigo in GC 4412 and has been continuously funded since. Its published output to date is 2,411 registered patents and eleven classes of working device.

Public reception of that work is recorded in the member consultations as consistently unfavourable and consistently qualified. Respondents oppose the reconstruction of autonomous systems in substantial majority and, asked separately, describe the loss of them as the change they most regret.

**Character.** Coastal, mild, and organised around the university. The world has no significant industry not attached to it, and no significant politics not conducted within it.

**Religious note.** The tenets catalogued in this volume as Simulationism are concentrated here and are professed disproportionately among senior faculty and their endowment donors. Saigo does not treat this as a religious matter and files no observance.
