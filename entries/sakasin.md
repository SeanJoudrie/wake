# SAKASIN
`RECOVERED`

A world Castle Team deployed to, remembered by exactly one of them for exactly one reason.

**What happened there.** A round went through Berji Kegeron's shoulder. He lived, the wound closed, and by every measure that the Guard records it was an unremarkable engagement on an unremarkable rotation.

**Why it is in this archive at all.** Because he has never stopped taking it apart. Not the pain of it. The geometry.

A few inches higher and the round takes his head instead. He has traced every choice he made that morning, every habit, every unconscious shift of weight, and every choice the man on the other end made across his entire life, and concluded that the two paths were always going to intersect at that exact point in the air.

Whether that is insight or damage is not a question the record can settle. But the shoulder healed, and the thought did not.
