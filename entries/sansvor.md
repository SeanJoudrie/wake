# SANSVOR
*local pronunciation renders the name in one and a half syllables*
`CONTESTED`

Mild temperate world of a substantial pre-war industrial and civic build, presently unmaintained. Blockaded during the war. Not a member.

**System:** Sansvor (single star, ten bodies, one habitable)
**Status:** independent. Not a member. No standing garrison. Guard consular presence since GC 4433
**Population:** between 100 million and 600 million
**Membership:** residence. There is no formal instrument of membership and no authority empowered to issue one
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar in the ports; at least four local reckonings in concurrent civil use, none of them official
**Principal exports:** none of significance. Scholarship, salvage, emigrant labour

**Fabric.** The world was built out extensively in the century before the war: rail, airship terminals, cathedrals, libraries, civic halls, cut stone and heavy timber. Very little of it was destroyed. Almost all of it is unmaintained. The characteristic condition of Sansvor is a sound structure with an open roof.

The climate permits this. Winters are mild. Buildings that would be uninhabitable elsewhere remain in daily use here with a wall missing.

**Population.** Literacy returns, where local authorities still produce them, remain among the highest in this archive. The universities did not close. Their endowments failed, their faculties were not paid, and a substantial proportion continued teaching. Museum and library holdings are largely intact and largely uncatalogued.

**Politics.** Fragmented. The Restoration counts between nineteen and thirty organised political associations of significance, the range reflecting disagreement over what counts as one. The disputes recorded in their literature concern the pre-war order and its succession. They do not concern food.

**The blockade.** `CONTESTED`, and the point on which this entry is filed as such.

*Account A.* Guard material, and the account given by a majority of informants in the port settlements, holds that the blockade was imposed by a belligerent party during the war and was broken by Guard action in GC 4409, and that the Guard is therefore properly remembered here as the relieving force.

*Account B.* A minority of informants, concentrated in the interior, hold that the interdiction of the world's shipping was continued after the cessation of hostilities in order to establish the conditions under which relief would later be requested.

The manifests that would settle the question are port manifests, and the port record for GC 4402 to GC 4409 is not in this archive. Applications have been made. `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`
