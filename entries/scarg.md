# "SCARG"
`INTACT`

Rubbish. Refuse. What you throw out.

An ordinary word, in ordinary use, on every world that speaks Standard. It carries no charge and nobody flinches at it.

**Except.** In a galaxy where nothing can be built any more, the line between rubbish and stock is a matter of who is looking. A dealer in pre-war components and a man carrying a sack to the tip are handling the same objects and using different words for them.

So the word does quiet work. Something is scarg until somebody who knows better sees it, and then it is not, and then it never was.

There are people who make a living entirely in that gap.
