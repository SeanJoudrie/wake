# SCHEDULE OF UNREPLACEABLE CLASSES
`INTACT`

An insurance instrument. The schedule of item classes for which no production facility is known to survive, maintained by the underwriters of the member worlds and adopted by the Guard for valuation purposes.

**Purpose.** An item that cannot be replaced cannot be indemnified by replacement. The schedule exists so that such items can be valued and insured, and it is revised annually as facilities are confirmed lost or, very occasionally, confirmed found.

**Current schedule, principal classes:** plasma weapons and their charge assemblies. Personal armour of quantum-threaded manufacture. Regenerative and cellular medicine. Vaccine stock of the pre-war formulations. Grav-rudder assemblies. Gravity spire regulators. Slipstream drives above class 4. Autonomous and remote systems of every class. Personal transport of the repulsor type. Agricultural combine drive units. Maldo steel and its alloys. Certified parent seed lines.

**Movement in the schedule.** Additions in the last twenty years: 41. Deletions in the last twenty years: 3, all three following certification of reconstructed manufacture at Saigo.

**Consequence for valuation.** Items on the schedule appreciate against every other class of property. The underwriters' index for scheduled classes has risen in every year it has been published. The Restoration notes, without further comment, that a weapon is therefore among the most reliable stores of value available to an ordinary person, and that the largest holder of scheduled items in the galaxy is the Guard.
