# SCHEDULE OF UNREPLACEABLE CLASSES
`INTACT`

The insurers' list of things that cannot be made any more.

It exists for a boring reason. You cannot indemnify something by replacing it if there is no replacement, so underwriters had to write down what falls into that category in order to price it. The list gets revised every year.

**What is on it.** Plasma weapons and their charge assemblies. Quantum-thread armour. Regenerative medicine. Pre-war vaccine stock. Grav-rudder assemblies. Gravity spire regulators. Slipstream drives above class 4. Every kind of drone and remote system. Repulsor transport. Combine drive units. Maldo steel. Certified parent seed lines.

Forty-one additions in the last twenty years. Three deletions, all three after Saigo certified it could build the thing again.

**What it means.** Everything on this list appreciates against everything not on it, permanently, because the supply only ever falls. Which is why a rifle is a better store of value than money out on the rim, and why the largest holder of scheduled items in the galaxy is the Guard.
