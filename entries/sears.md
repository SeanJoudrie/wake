# SEARS
`CONTESTED`

One line, spoken to him rather than by him.

> *"This better work, Sears."*

That is the entire record. From it you can extract that he exists, that he is the one doing something technical enough to fail, and that the man relying on him is not confident.

Everything else about Sears is somebody else's guess.
