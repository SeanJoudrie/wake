# THE SEASON OF LONG NIGHTS
`TESTIMONY`

The Maspin year turns on it. A season of rebirth, of retribution, and of relighting the fire everybody shares.

The last night of it is sacred.

We do not know how long it lasts, when it falls, or what happens during it. Our informants would not describe the rites and we did not push. What they would say is what it is for, and they said all three of those words every time, in that order, as though the sequence mattered.
