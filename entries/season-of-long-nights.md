# THE SEASON OF LONG NIGHTS
`TESTIMONY`

Maspin observance. A period of rebirth, of retribution, and of the rekindling of the shared fire. Its closing night is sacred.

Duration, dating, and the content of the rites are not recorded here. Our informants declined to describe them and the Restoration did not press.

Attributed to D., Riflo, and to two others.
