# SERGEANT AREN
`INTACT`

Guard military police, and the most forgettable man in the service.

That is not our assessment. It is what people say about him, unprompted and in almost identical language: silent, meek, and the sort who would disappear into the background entirely if he were not wearing the uniform.

He is police. He is present at a great many things. Nobody remembers him being there.
