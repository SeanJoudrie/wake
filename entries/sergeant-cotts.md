# SERGEANT COTTS
`INTACT`

Guard military police. Grey hair, tall, and an aura that quietly dares you to test her patience.

> *She may have been tall, but was always known to be short with people.*

She has the kind of pride that does not bend, including on the occasions when bending would clearly be the better idea. Everyone knows this about her and nobody says it to her face.

She is used, by people senior to her, as a warning. A Director telling a soldier not to let anyone like Sergeant Cotts see a particular thing is not making a joke, and the soldier does not take it as one.
