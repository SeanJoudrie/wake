# SERSHANTI RAWLDEN
*formerly Kinsley*
`RECOVERED`

Wife of Grai Penston Rawlden. She holds no office and is not a member of the cabinet.

**The marriage.** It predates his authority and has continued through it. Rawlden conducted an affair, acknowledged it rather than denying it, and the two separated for a period before reconciling.

He offered her a divorce. She refused it.

Accounts from people close to the household agree on her reasons and none of them is affection. A separation would have removed her from the position she holds, which is proximity to the only authority on the world, and she judged that she was better served by remaining in it. The marriage has been presented publicly as a stable one ever since, by both of them, and neither has ever been recorded departing from that presentation.

**What she does with it.** She is described as influential without being formally anything. Where and how is not established. Several informants who deal with the administration state independently that a view known to be hers is treated with more care than a view known to be a minister's, and none of them was able to give an example.

**On the son.** She has never been recorded speaking about Dorch Rawlden in public or in private, favourably or otherwise. He carries her married name.
