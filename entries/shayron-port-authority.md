# SHAYRON PORT AUTHORITY
`INTACT`

The people who check you in on the capital world, and the only officials on Shayron who look bored.

Two of them at the gate, pulse rifles strapped on lazily enough that the strap is doing more work than the hand. Between them a scanning ledger, which they run every arrival through and glance at afterwards rather than during.

The third one is the useful one: a guard who gives directions. Sidor Avenue, then Alvion Street, then Park Square. He says it in the order you will walk it, without being asked twice, which suggests he says it forty times a day.
