# SHEEL
`INTACT`

Worked moon of the Polaris system. Meat, and the rendering operations on which Weton's soil depends.

**System:** Polaris
**Status:** local government retained. Licensing held on Kelton
**Population:** between 2 million and 15 million
**Membership:** application and assessment
**Calendars in use:** Polaris Harvest Reckoning
**Principal exports:** meat, bone meal, blood meal, manure, tallow

Grassland and vat towers. Filed output GC 4461: 40,116,200 tonnes carcass weight, 18,904,700 tonnes rendered fertiliser product.

The fertiliser is the consequential half. Weton's yields are set by Sheel's rendering volume with a lag of approximately eighteen months, a relationship visible in the yield returns of both bodies and stated in no contract between them. The Restoration has found no external commentary on it in any source.
