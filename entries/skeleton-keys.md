# SKELETON KEYS
`RECOVERED`

A pre-war device that copies everything off a terminal without touching it.

You put it anywhere on the housing and it sticks, magnetically. No port, no cable, no connection of any kind. Then you wait, because it is not fast, and when the indicator turns it is finished.

**Nothing detects them.** Every security system now in service was built after the Wipe by people who had never seen one and had nothing to design against. Guardnet's published protections do not list the class, because as far as its architects were concerned the class does not exist.

Almost none survive. They are not insured, not scheduled, and not traded openly, and the technical material we hold breaks off after the processor generation.
