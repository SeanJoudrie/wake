# SKELETON KEYS
`RECOVERED`

A pre-war device for the copying of data from a terminal without authorisation and without record.

**Operation, as described.** The device is placed in contact with any part of a terminal housing and adheres magnetically. No connection is made and no port is used. Copying begins after a delay and is signalled on completion by an indicator on the device.

**Detection.** None. The devices predate the security architecture of every system now in service, and the reconstructed systems were built without knowledge of them. Guardnet's published security schedules do not list the class.

**Stock.** Unknown and believed very small. The class does not appear in the underwriters' schedule of unreplaceable classes, which the Restoration attributes to the fact that no underwriter has been asked to insure one.

`[RECORD DAMAGED]` The recoverable technical material describes the processor generation and stops.
