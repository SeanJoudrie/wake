# "SLAVE-FREE TERRITORY"
`INTACT`

A form of declaration adopted by a number of worlds and territories, certifying the absence of slavery within their jurisdiction.

**The instrument.** A short declaration in standard form, filed with the Guard, reciting the prohibition in Galactic Standard law and certifying compliance. It requires no inspection, provides for no verification, and is granted on filing.

**Effect.** The declaration is accurate. Slavery, as defined in the code, is not practised in the declaring jurisdictions. The code defines slavery as the holding of a person as property.

**What the declaration does not address.** Indenture, in which the person is not property and the obligation is to a debt. Custodial labour, in which the obligation is to a sentence. Levy, in which the obligation is to an instrument. None of these is slavery within the definition, and each is separately lawful.

**Adopters.** 61 jurisdictions have filed the declaration. Of those, 44 record lawful indenture in their own returns for the same year.

**Drafting.** The standard form was drafted on Maldo. Its structure, its recitals and its certifying clause follow the pattern of Royal ministerial instruments of the same period, several of which are held here for comparison.
