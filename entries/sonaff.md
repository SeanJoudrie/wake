# SONAFF
`TESTIMONY`

Frozen through, dark almost all of the time, and lit from above by an aurora that does not stop. There is nothing here. That is the whole of its strategic value and the whole of its history.

**System:** filed as Sonaff in the basing schedules. The primary is not named in any document we hold `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`
**Status:** Guard installation. No civil administration
**Population:** no permanent civil population. Garrison strength is not published
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar
**Principal exports:** none

**What it is for.** Sonaff appears in Guard planning as a waypoint and a place to leave things. It has no assessed resource value. Nobody has ever contested it because nobody has ever wanted it.

**Why it matters anyway.** Being worthless made it reachable. Missionary orders came here early and kept coming, and a number of the populations out on those moons, including populations that will not tolerate contact of any other kind, hold faiths that arrived from Sonaff.

That is the most consequential thing about this moon and it appears in no official filing.

**Under the ice.** Two survey teams have reported an anomalous mass beneath it. Neither report was followed up. We hold both, in summary. `[RECORD DAMAGED]`
