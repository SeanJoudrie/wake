# SONAFF
`TESTIMONY`

Frozen moon. Effectively permanent night. Guard garrison presence, small and long-standing.

**System:** filed as Sonaff in Guard basing schedules. The primary is not named in any document held here `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`
**Status:** Guard installation. No civil administration
**Population:** no permanent civil population is recorded. Garrison strength is not published
**Membership:** not applicable
**Calendars in use:** Galactic Calendar
**Principal exports:** none

**Character.** Frozen through. Aurora is continuous and is the principal source of surface light. The moon appears in Guard planning documents as a waypoint and a storage location, and in no other capacity. It has no assessed resource value.

**Missionary activity.** The remoteness that made the moon useless made it attractive to missionary orders, which reached it early and repeatedly. A number of the surrounding populations, including populations otherwise hostile to all contact, hold faiths transmitted from Sonaff. This is the most substantial thing about the moon and appears in no official filing.

**Subsurface.** Two survey teams have reported an anomalous mass beneath the ice. Neither report was followed up. Both are held here in summary. `[RECORD DAMAGED]`
