# SPACE AIRBAGS
`INTACT`

The last thing a ship does, and it does it without being asked.

When a vessel's systems work out that the situation is no longer recoverable, they fire the reservoirs. Expanding foam floods the hull in seconds, filling every compartment, every passage, and every space around every crew member, setting to a dense semi-rigid mass that holds a body still through an impact that would otherwise fold it.

It dissolves shortly afterwards. Not on a timer, and not on a command. It simply stops being foam, sags, and drains away, leaving a residue on the deck and a survivor who is generally unconscious.

**The unsettling part.** The ship makes the decision. There is no lever. By the time a pilot has understood that the situation is unsurvivable, the foam has usually already gone off, which means most people who have been through it did not experience the crash at all. They experienced a pressure, then nothing, then waking up.

Crews are not warm about this. A machine that knows before you do, and acts before you can, is a machine that has already had an opinion about your competence.
