# SPARA AVARIS
`INTACT`

A decorated Royal Space Force pilot who left the service, married a chemist, and raised two boys on an asteroid.

Three decorations, each with a citation, and then a discharge at her own request and a life nobody in the Royal systems ever heard about again. She kept her service pistol, which is regular and permitted, and she kept it clean.

**The stories.** She told her younger son about Modanick, where he was born and had never lived. Always warm. Always daytime. Trees taller than houses. She had loved it there as a student and she made it better than it was, because she was describing a green world to a child who lived on a rock, and that is what you do.

He believed all of it.

**The last thing she did.** She got her pistol and she killed one of them. There were two.

She fell across a floorboard, which turned out to matter.
