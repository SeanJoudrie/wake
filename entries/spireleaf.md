# SPIRELEAF
`INTACT`

A low, broad-leaved plant that will grow under a gravity spire, which almost nothing will.

The light under an envelope is wrong and the pressure gradient is worse, and most cultivars simply give up. This one does not. It came out of somebody's greenhouse a long time ago and it is now on every asteroid colony in the field.

It appears in the supply manifests as a seed issue, listed under gardening rather than food. Which means people living on a rock, breathing borrowed air, asked for something to look at, and somebody put it on the ship.
