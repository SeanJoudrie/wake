# SPIRELEAF
`INTACT`

A low broad-leaved plant cultivated under gravity spire atmosphere. Tolerant of the light spectrum and the pressure gradients characteristic of spire envelopes, in which most cultivars fail.

It appears in the supply schedules of asteroid habitation as a seed issue and in the same schedules as a gardening item rather than a food. The Restoration takes this to mean it was grown to be looked at.
