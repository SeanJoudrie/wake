# SQUAD 412
`CONTESTED`

An ordinary Guard rifle squad, and the most consequential thing anybody ever filmed.

They did nothing that hundreds of other squads have not done. They finished a job, they went through what was left of it under Standing Order 41, and they celebrated. Loudly. With the take spread out in front of them.

**The problem was the camera.** A Royal journalist was embedded with them, shooting morale footage. Guardsmen laughing, comrades in good spirits, the aftermath of a successful operation. That is what he thought he was making. Everybody in the frame thought so too.

Standing Order 41 is entirely lawful. It is also entirely unfilmed, by long custom, because the difference between an authorised recovery and a sack of somebody's property is largely a matter of how it looks.

Nobody in that squad has been charged with anything, and the reason is that no charge is available. Which leaves the footage as the only problem, and footage is a much simpler kind of problem to solve than men are.
