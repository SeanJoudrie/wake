# THE BLIND DAY
`TESTIMONY`

Blindfolded, cuffed together, and turned loose for a full day in front of everybody they have ever known.

**The rite.** The couple is bound at the wrist and blinded, and must live an entire ordinary day like that. Eat. Move. Get from one place to another. Every single thing requires the other person, and neither can see what the other is doing.

Two things are being said at once, and the rite does not bother to separate them: *your fate is chained to theirs now*, and *you will have to trust this person when you have nothing left to go on.*

**The families set the tasks**, and the tasks are meant to be funny rather than cruel. This is the community's one licensed opportunity to make the couple look ridiculous, and it is taken up enthusiastically. Cook a meal for both families, neither of you able to see the fire. Cross running water on stones. Carry something fragile across town. Find an object a sibling has hidden, and the sibling is permitted to lie exactly once.

**The last task is dressing each other.** Ceremonial clothes are laid out, and each dresses the other blind.

They walk into the ceremony wearing whatever they have made of one another, and nobody straightens a thing. The crooked collar is the point. Every wedding portrait on that world has one.

**Then the blindfolds come off.** That is the first time they see each other married, and it is where the ceremony *begins*. The whole day was the prelude.

**Peeking is not an annulment.** It is worse. It is remembered.
