# THE COAL RUN
`TESTIMONY`

A heated span with water on both sides, and the couple starts at opposite ends.

**The rite.** They run toward each other. The marriage exists the instant their lips meet in the middle. No words. Nobody officiates. Either it happened or it did not, and every person present saw the exact moment.

**Opposite ends is the entire design.** Either one of them can stop. If one stops, the other is left standing alone on the far side, in front of everybody, and there is no version of that which can be smoothed over afterward. Both have to keep going. That is the vow, and it is made with the legs.

**The surface** is something genuinely unpleasant and genuinely survivable, and it varies with what the world has: superheated grating over an exhaust manifold, plasma venting on a timed cycle so that the run has a window, or a cooling Corefuel channel, which is to say they run across the thing the galaxy is powered by.

**The falling.** Usually one of them goes in the water. This is the part that makes the culture.

The other jumps in after them. Voluntarily, immediately, and without being asked. And then everybody else wades in and carries the pair of them across.

Nobody is embarrassed. Nobody has failed. It is fun, it is half the reason people turn up, and the lesson gets stated by the crowd instead of by a priest: *you were never going to finish this on your own, and nobody expected you to.*

**A wedding where nobody fell in is considered a slightly boring wedding.**
