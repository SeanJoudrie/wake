# THE COLONY RECOVERY DETAIL
`RECOVERED`

The Guard element that walked the Corefuel colony after the raid, and the two men in it who matter.

**The soldier who lifted the floorboard.** He did it because it was the next board. He was not searching for anybody. That is the whole thing: a routine sweep of a burned house, done properly, by somebody who had no reason to expect anything under there.

**The officer.** He read the station chief off a scan, marked him killed in action, and closed the family out on an assumption spoken flatly enough that it was clearly not the first time he had said it.

> *"There's probably not even a body left after those ion bombs."*

He was wrong about one of them.
