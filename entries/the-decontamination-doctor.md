# THE DECONTAMINATION DOCTOR
`TESTIMONY`

Whoever is on shift at the far end of the Maspin decon cycle, waiting for you to be finished being sick.

The cycle is thorough. Most first-timers come out of it on their knees, and this one has watched enough of that to have a routine: he waits, he lets you get there yourself, and then he offers a hand and a smirk he does not fully suppress.

> *"First time, I take it? Won't take long to get used to. Mind over matter, unless you've got a severe parasite, scales, or plasma burns."*

The list at the end is the useful part. Those three are the ones that do not get better with practice.
