# THE DETONATION
`TESTIMONY`

At the moment of the union, something is destroyed, loudly, on purpose.

A cannon. An enormous firework. A structure brought down under control, chosen in advance and often chosen carefully. Whatever the world can manage, the requirement is that it is sudden, that it is loud, and that it cannot be undone.

**It is not celebratory noise.** That is the thing outsiders get wrong. This is joy deliberately punctured, at its own high point, by the memory of catastrophe. The bang is not applause. It is a correction.

**What it says.** *Nothing lasts. This will not last either. We are doing it anyway.*

On worlds that came out of the war with a continent bombed flat, or a capital that is now a beautiful ruin with the sky coming in through it, the rite is not symbolic and nobody there needs it explained. Everyone in the room has seen the real version of what is being staged.

**And the sound is the memory.** Which means at every wedding on such a world, there is a portion of the crowd who flinch, who know they are going to flinch, who came anyway, and who are held by somebody standing next to them for a second afterward.
