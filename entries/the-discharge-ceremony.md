# THE DISCHARGE CEREMONY
`INTACT`

The Guard does not simply stop paying you. It gathers everybody up and says so out loud.

**Two venues.** Either an auditorium aboard the mothership, which is efficient and means nobody travels, or a proper ceremony on the ground.

The ground option is held on one of the junction worlds. Not a capital and not anywhere important, just one of the places that exists mostly as a place other places connect through, with routes running everywhere and nothing much at the centre of it. They are chosen for exactly that: it is the shortest total journey for the largest number of families, which is the sort of decision this organisation is extremely good at making.

Outdoors, when the weather on that world permits. Rows of chairs. A stage. Names read out.

**What it is for.** A contract ending is a commercial event and there is no reason at all to hold an event about it. They hold one anyway, because a soldier who leaves quietly tells people it was nothing, and a soldier who leaves in front of his mother tells them it was something.

Several people in this record are within a year of standing in that field.
