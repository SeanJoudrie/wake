# THE FRIEND
*the Maspin shaman*
`TESTIMONY`

Ask for the shaman on Maspin and you will be corrected. He would rather you called him Friend.

He smiles in a way people describe, without prompting and in almost the same words, as soft and ancient.

**What he does.** Nobody outside is clear. He is present at the festival, he is consulted about people who are dying, and the Trailblazer surgeon at Base Delta credits him with stopping a man from bleeding out through a hole in the heart, and then walking that man back to a vehicle afterwards.

**Finding him.** You do not. Ask a Maspin child where the shaman is and you will get a straight answer:

> *"You don't find the shaman. The shaman finds you."*

**Two things that unsettle visitors.** He knows names he has not been told. And he has been known to leave something at the medbay for a person before that person arrived on the world, with instructions about who to say it came from.
