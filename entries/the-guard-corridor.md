# THE GUARD CORRIDOR
`INTACT`

A hallway on the mothership lined with small chapels, one per faith, rentable by the hour.

**How it works.** The paperwork is the marriage. You file first, at a desk, and you are married when the clerk stamps it. Everything after that is a service you are purchasing.

Then you pick a door. There are a great many doors. The enlistment rites form lists eighty-eight faiths and the corridor makes a decent effort at all of them, which means a stretch of identical hatchways opening onto rooms that share nothing but their dimensions: incense in one, water in the next, a bare wooden bench, a stained-glass panel lit from behind, an altar with a sun on it.

**There is a price list.** It is posted. It is not expensive and it is not free, and the difference between the rooms is entirely a matter of how much is in them.

**The room at the end has nothing in it.** No symbol, no furniture, no fee.

It is the busiest room in the corridor by a wide margin, and it has been for as long as the mothership has been flying. Some of that is money. Some of it is eighty-eight faiths and none of them being yours. And some of it is two people who have thirty-one hours of shore leave and no interest in explaining themselves to anybody.
