# THE GUARD FRANCHISE
`INTACT`

Everybody votes, the votes are counted honestly, and the count is published. There is no fraud in this entry.

Every Guard citizen of age gets one vote. Citizenship of your own planet gets you nothing here; you can hold local office on it and still not vote in a Guard poll. Registers are kept by the worlds and audited by the Guard, and fifty-three years of returns exist for every world and district, with the question printed in full.

**What gets voted on.** Over the last twenty years: 88 polls on roads and transit works, 61 on drug licensing, 44 on municipal boundaries, 39 on holidays and observance dates, 31 on harbour works, 22 on schools, and 106 on assorted local statute.

**What does not.** Appropriations. Licensing. Who sits on the Board. Standing orders. Whether an operation happens.

Turnout averages 79.4 per cent and has been climbing for a decade. People vote because voting works. The things they vote on are the things nobody upstairs minds losing.
