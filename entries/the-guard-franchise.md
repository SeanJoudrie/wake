# THE GUARD FRANCHISE
`INTACT`

The electoral system of the member worlds. Universal, direct, and in continuous operation since GC 4409.

**Entitlement.** Every Guard citizen of age holds one vote. Planetary citizenship confers no vote. Persons holding planetary citizenship without Guard citizenship may hold office locally and may not vote in a Guard poll.

**Conduct.** Registers are maintained by the member worlds and audited by the Guard. Polls are conducted on a fixed cycle. Turnout, results, spoiled papers, and register size are filed for every poll ever held.

**The record.** This is among the most complete document series in this archive. Fifty-three years of returns, by world, by district, with the question put in full.

**Subject matter.** The questions put, GC 4442 to GC 4461, by category: highway and transit works, 88; licensing of recreational substances, 61; municipal boundary alteration, 44; observance and holiday scheduling, 39; port and harbour works, 31; educational statute, 22; miscellaneous local statute, 106.

Appropriation has not been put. Licensing has not been put. Membership of the Board has not been put. Standing orders have not been put. Operational authorisation has not been put.

**Participation.** Mean turnout across the period: 79.4 per cent. The Restoration records that this is high, that it is rising, and that both facts are true.
