# THE GUARD NURSES
`RECOVERED`

Two of them, on the same ward, and the most frightening thing about them is how ordinary the work is.

A man comes off a transport screaming and thrashing. They take an arm each and pin him to a thin mattress, and they do it the way two people carry a table through a doorway they have carried tables through before. No hesitation, no signal between them, nothing said.

Then the respirator, then the sedative, then the next one.
