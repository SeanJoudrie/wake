# THE HALGO FAMILY
`TESTIMONY`

Maspin locals near the Trailblazer base, and the household the medevac dispatcher guesses first.

Nothing about them is on record except that. When a call comes in from that stretch of country, the voice on the other end says, without checking anything:

> *"Is it the Halgo family again?"*

Which tells you the shape of them without a single name. Somebody in that house is always doing something. It has been going on long enough that a bored woman on a radio has stopped needing to look it up.
