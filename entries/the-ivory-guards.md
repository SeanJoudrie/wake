# THE IVORY GUARDS
`INTACT`

The ceremonial line on Shayron, and the most photographed soldiers in the galaxy.

Pristine ivory armour. Long ceremonial coats that fall past the knee. Helmets built tall enough to add most of a head, kept immaculate, and never once seen with a scuff on them.

They stand exactly fifty metres apart. Not roughly. Every gap on the approach is the same gap, and the sameness is doing the work: a line of men that regular reads as a fact about the place rather than a decision anyone made.

Whether they can fight is a question nobody asks, which is the point.
