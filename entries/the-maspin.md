# THE MASPIN
`TESTIMONY`

Non-human people of Maspin. Bodies of smoke and vapour. Approximately toddler height in the general population.

**Physiology, as described.** No solid form. Eyes described consistently as bodies of water. An ember or embers within the head. Features that shift with disposition, which informants of other species report reading with practice and not before. No mouth. Speech is described as emerging from within rather than from a point.

Maspin produce sparks from the fingers sufficient to light tinder. This is universal and is not remarked on locally.

**Diet.** Absorption of plant byproduct, supplemented by liquid and near-liquid food. Catalogued separately.

**Family.** The Restoration has not established the structure. Informants describe the whole people as one family and decline further specification, and it is not clear to us whether this is a figure of speech, a statement of kinship practice, or a statement of what they believe themselves to be. We record the ambiguity rather than resolve it.

**Death.** The Maspin hold that they are constituted from the subterranean ocean and return to it. The interment rite is catalogued separately.

**Economy.** Fruit cultivation and export, kraskor herding, floom husbandry, port labour under the Trailblazer concession, temple and civic service, and the professions. Our informants included a chemist, a caretaker, a councillor, and a clerk who keeps the tide tables for the Riflo port and asked us twice whether we needed anything else.

**Labour status.** Indentured servitude is lawful on Maspin and is administered by the Council. Terms and enforcement are catalogued separately.
