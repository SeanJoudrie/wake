# THE MEDBAY DOCTOR
`RECOVERED`

A Guard physician on a busy ward, working at the speed of a man who has done this exact thing all week.

He is not unkind. He is simply not present. He reads a file the way you read a delivery manifest, glances at the body attached to it, and gives instructions to the room rather than to the patient.

> *"Some fight in this one. You, grab the gas."*

The bedside manner arrives as a joke, and it is meant well, and it lands like a dropped tray.

> *"You'll be back to killing Crags in no time."*
