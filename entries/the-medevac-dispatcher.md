# THE MEDEVAC DISPATCHER
`TESTIMONY`

A woman's voice on the far end of Calsco's radio, and the flattest, most reassuring sound in the sector.

She does not ask what happened. She asks whose house it is. Emergency work on a small colony eventually becomes neighbourhood work, and after enough years the two are the same job.

> *"Is it the Halgo family again?"*
