# THE MILITARY POLICE
`INTACT`

Not a duty roster. A service, with its own ladder, its own specialisations, and a doctrine that is simpler than anybody expects.

**What is in it.** Detectives working actual cases. Riot control. Border and port guards. Prison staff. Investigators who do nothing but paperwork and are worse news than the ones who carry batons. It is a developed institution with a career in it, and a man can spend thirty years inside it without ever standing at a gate.

**What they can do, on paper.** Enter any quarters. Detain anybody. Charge anybody.

**What they can do in practice.** Whatever their rank supports.

A newly qualified MP who walks unannounced into the quarters of a special operations captain will discover that the corridor, the duty officer, and every witness available respects the captain considerably more than it respects him, and that the incident goes badly in a way no regulation predicted. To touch somebody at that height you go up your own chain first and let your captain of military police make the request. Everybody involved knows this. It is not written down anywhere.

**The doctrine.** They care about instability. That is the whole of it.

Get drunk enough to be a nuisance and you will be told to keep yourself together, by somebody who has said it four hundred times and does not care what you do next. Bring something in from a world you were not supposed to bring it in from, some restricted cheese or a bottle or a plant, and you get a warning and your afternoon back. They genuinely do not care.

Do something that threatens the stability of the arrangement and you have an opponent, permanently, with detectives and prisons attached.

The rules are not what they enforce. Order is.
