# THE MOTHERSHIP
`INTACT`

Principal mobile establishment of the Guard. Carries operational, administrative, medical, judicial and residential functions in one hull.

**Rotation.** The vessel maintains a fixed circular rotation of the member worlds, filed annually in advance and published. The full circuit takes rather more than a standard year. Deployment of embarked units is a function of where the rotation currently is, which produces long busy periods and long quiet ones in a pattern anyone serving can predict a year ahead.

**Composition.** The Guard is not organised in separate branches. Flight, ground, engineering, medical, judicial and administrative divisions share the same hull, the same messes and the same establishment. Commodores and ground ranks appear on the same seniority list.

**Filed particulars:** complement, berthing, magazine capacity, medical bed state, rated endurance and consumption are all published in the annual establishment return and are precise.
