# THE MOTHERSHIP
`INTACT`

The Guard's home. A single hull carrying operations, administration, medicine, courts, workshops and everybody's quarters, permanently on the move.

It runs a fixed circuit of the member worlds, published a year in advance, and takes rather more than a standard year to get all the way round. That circuit governs everyone's life aboard: when it is in a busy sector you deploy every couple of weeks, and when it is not, you have a month with nothing to do. People plan around it the way other people plan around seasons.

There are no separate branches. Pilots, infantry, engineers, doctors, clerks and the judge advocate share the same corridors, the same messes and the same seniority list. A commodore and a staff sergeant queue for the same food.

Somebody described it as being like college, in that you have free time and choose what to do with it, and the timetable is still pointed straight at you.
