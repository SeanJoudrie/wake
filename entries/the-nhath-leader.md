# THE NHATH LEADER
`INFERRED`

A Nhath of high lineage number, contracting party to eleven Guard engagements. His name is in the contracts. We are not printing it, because the only other place it appears is a list of people the Restoration has been asked not to identify.

**Lineage.** Recorded. Three figures. That is eighty and more generations of a family kept in base eight by a people the Guard's own ministerial material describes as having no political organisation.

**Payment.** In full, on the scheduled date, eleven times across nineteen years. The disbursing fund is identified by an internal reference which we have not been able to resolve. `[FILING INCOMPLETE]`

**The chip.** Guard translator hardware is serialised, and the serial ranges are allocated by production quarter and are filed, which means any recovered unit can be dated to within three months.

A unit is described in testimony as worn at the neck by this individual. If that description is accurate, the implant postdates the earliest of his Guard engagements and was bought, on the balance of the payment record, with money that came out of that fund.

**The scar.** The medical schedule for the implant notes that placement at the neck leaves a characteristic mark, and that the procedure is sought by patients wishing to cover a mark already in that place.

We have set that note beside a discharge record from the Royal Space Force elsewhere in this volume. We are not able to join them and we are not able to stop noticing them.
