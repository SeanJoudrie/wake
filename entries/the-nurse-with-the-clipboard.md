# THE NURSE WITH THE CLIPBOARD
`TESTIMONY`

Base Delta, waiting room, the one who calls the next name.

She steps in with the clipboard already tilted, reads what is written on it, and does not react to the fact that the man it belongs to is dressed as a squire.

> *"Mister... Squire? We're ready for you."*

The pause before the name is the entire performance. She has decided, in that half second, that whatever this is, it is not her business and the room is running behind.
