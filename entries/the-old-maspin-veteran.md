# THE OLD MASPIN VETERAN
`TESTIMONY`

An old Maspin, met in passing, who fought in the war and has been thinking about it ever since.

> *"These kids have it easy nowadays."*
> *"Maybe not easy. Maybe I'm just bitter. I grew up during the war."*
> *"Some days, it feels like nothing's changed. I thought fighting would fix our problems, but here we are. Other times, it feels like everything's changed, and I'm just a fish out of water."*

That is the whole conversation. It is also, as far as this archive can tell, the most complete account anybody has given us of what the war did to the generation that came through it.
