# THE PALACE STAFF
`TESTIMONY`

The people who keep the Palace of Riflo running, and who open its doors by hand because the doors have always been opened by hand.

**The red-robed pair.** Two figures at the great oak doors, and the thing you notice first is that they are made of smoke rather than wrapped in it. The robe is the outer edge of the person, not a garment over one. They take a leaf each and pull, and the weight of the oak is real enough that it takes a moment.

**The attendant.** An alien in flowing robes who bows without speaking, receives the instruction, and leaves to fetch appetisers. The bow is not deference exactly. It is the practised economy of somebody who has decided that talking is the slower way to do this.
