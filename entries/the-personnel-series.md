# THE PERSONNEL SERIES
`INTACT`

Why this volume can tell you the colour of a soldier's visor.

A Guard records depot was closed down and its paper duplicates were released for disposal as superseded administrative material. The Restoration collected them. It was lawful and it was almost certainly not intended.

The Guard files its people in extraordinary detail: colouring, height, old injuries, what kit they were issued, which faith they wrote on the form at seventeen, who gets the money if they die. Most of what this volume knows about anyone currently serving comes out of that one shipment.

It runs to a single date, which is the day the depot last bundled its paper, and stops.
