# THE PERSONNEL SERIES
`INTACT`

A partial run of Guard personnel files, on paper, held by this archive. It is the reason there is a persons section in this volume at all, and it is the reason that section reads the way it does.

**How we came by it.** A regional records depot was decommissioned and its paper duplicates were released for disposal. The Restoration collected them. The transfer was lawful, was documented, and was almost certainly not intended, the schedule describing the contents as superseded administrative material.

**What is in it.** Everything. The Guard files its people to a standard nothing else in this archive approaches: identification and medical schedules with height, colouring and distinguishing marks; equipment issue down to the visor; postings, commendations, disciplinary notes, pay grade, leave taken, and the rites recorded at enrolment.

Where the series covers a person, we can tell you the shape of their nose.

**Where it stops.** The duplicates run to a single cut-off date, which is the date the depot's paper was last bundled. There is nothing after it. Not a summary, not a closing note, not a transfer out.

Every file in the series simply ends on the same day, in the middle of whatever the person was doing.

*[Compiler's note: we have read all of these. They are complete, exact, and they stop, and the stopping is the part that stays with you. A man's eye colour is in here. What happened to him is not.]*
