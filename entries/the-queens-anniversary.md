# THE QUEEN'S ANNIVERSARY
`INTACT`

Principal observance of the Royal calendar. Held on Shayron, in the Capital District, on a fixed date.

**Form.** Procession along the processional way, address from the High Castle, public feeding, and evening rites in the quarters still in observance. Attendance is not ticketed. Crowd capacity of the way and the three principal squares is filed by the municipality at 214,000. Attendance in recent years is estimated by the municipality at above that figure.

**Character.** The observance is religious and civic at once, the crown being identified with Tracine rather than merely reigning by her favour. The distinction is catalogued under Tracineim and matters here only in that the day is not a birthday and is not treated as one.

**The vacancy.** The reigning queen has not appeared at the observance for four years. No announcement has been made. No death has been declared. The observance has been held in each of those years in full form, with the address given by the King.

The Restoration has been unable to establish the constitutional position and is satisfied that this is because there is not one. No instrument provides for the absence of a sovereign identified with a goddess, and no office holds the authority to declare such an absence ended or permanent.
