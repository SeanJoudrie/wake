# THE QUEEN'S ANNIVERSARY
`INTACT`

The biggest day in the Royal calendar, held in the Capital District on Shayron, on a fixed date.

A procession the length of the processional way, an address from the High Castle, food put out for everybody, and rites in the evening in the quarters still keeping them. Nobody is ticketed. The way and the three great squares hold two hundred and fourteen thousand people, and in recent years the city has stopped pretending the crowd is that small.

It is a religious day and a civic one at the same time, because the Queen is not merely reigning by Tracine's favour. She is held to be Tracine present on Shayron. It is not her birthday and nobody treats it as one.

**She has not been there for four years.** No announcement, no explanation, no death declared. The day has been held in full each of those years, with the King giving the address.

Nothing in Royal law covers the absence of a sovereign who is identified with a goddess. There is no office with the standing to say she is gone.
