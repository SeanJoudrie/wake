# THE REC ROOM
`INTACT`

A cavern of a room with a ceiling high enough to lose a ball in, and the loudest place on the ship that is not an engine.

The floor is waxed until it shines like liquid silver, and the walls are cheap warm wood, which fools nobody and works anyway. It sounds like a symphony badly conducted: laughter, the flat pock of moonballs on hardwood, the squeak of shoes, and the clang of dropped weights.

**One half is for sweating.** A moonball court, a weights area, and the grunting of men who have decided that today is the day.

**The other half is for not.** Booths and tables, vending machines dispensing snacks from six worlds, and screens running holo-movies nobody is really watching. Somebody usually has a bowl of luminescent starfruit out, which glows faintly like a handful of small nebulae and is mostly there to look at.

**The Zero-G Cue table**, tucked in a corner, is billiards adapted for low gravity, which means the balls travel forever and a badly judged shot is still travelling when your turn ends. It draws a small, obsessive crowd.

**And the cards.** Mead cards in partners, played for chips that mean nothing, at tables that stay occupied for eleven hours at a stretch.

It is the only room aboard where rank quietly stops mattering, which is not in any standing order and is enforced more strictly than most things that are.
