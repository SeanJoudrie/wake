# THE RELAY NETWORK
`INTACT`

Every word that crosses between stars crosses a tower, and the towers belong to churches.

**How that happened.** They built them to carry sermons. That was the entire purpose and nobody objected, because at the time there was a government, and infrastructure a faith paid for itself was infrastructure the government did not have to fund.

Then the government stopped existing. And in the years while everything else was being looted, burned or simply abandoned, eleven religious organisations defended their transmitters with everything they had, because to them the towers were not assets. They kept them. They still have them.

**What that means now.** There is no such thing as broadcasting in this galaxy. There is only buying airtime, by the hour, from one of eleven proprietors, at rates that vary by proprietor and by what is in the broadcast.

Music, news, sport, drama, education, weather, and the Guard's own morale material all compete for the same hours on the same towers, and the schedule is set by somebody whose actual job is theological.

**Two of the eleven refuse whole categories of material outright.** The refusals are in the correspondence and are not negotiable and have not moved in decades. Which means there are worlds where certain genres have simply never been heard, not because anybody banned them, but because the tower that reaches those worlds will not carry them.

**Nobody voted for this and nobody can undo it.** The towers are private property, lawfully held, defended at cost, and there is no authority in existence with the standing to take them.

The most powerful information organ in the galaxy is a customer.
