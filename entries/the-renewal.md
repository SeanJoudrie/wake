# THE RENEWAL
`TESTIMONY`

Married on a two-year term, and then you both decide again.

**The mechanism.** The marriage runs for a fixed period. At the end of it, either party renews or does not. Declining is not a divorce, carries no penalty, and requires no reason. You simply do not sign, and it is over, cleanly, that day.

**Inside a term it is strict.** Physical infidelity during a term is a criminal matter and is actually prosecuted, which sounds severe until you notice the logic: you had a door, it was clearly marked, and you chose not to use it.

**The stated intent** is that trust should be active rather than assumed. You are not married to somebody who has not left. You are married to somebody who chose this again, recently, with a clean exit available, and that is a materially different thing to be told.

**And that is exactly what makes it brutal.** There is always a door. Both of you know precisely when it opens next.

People count months. Everybody on that world counts months, and everybody pretends not to, and the last few weeks of a term are the loudest silence in any household there.

**The fertility clause**, where it survives, allows either party to decline without any social cost at all if a couple has wanted children and not managed them within a stated period. It is cold, it is practical, and it is almost certainly a post-war demographic measure that nobody has got round to repealing.
