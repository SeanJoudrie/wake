# THE RESCUE
`TESTIMONY`

Somebody's partner has been taken. Somebody is going to have to go and get them.

**The rite.** One of the couple is captured by the families, either one, and sometimes both at once. The other has to come and retrieve them, and between here and there is a gauntlet assembled entirely by people who have known them since they were small.

Swim the length of a pool while your relatives throw eggs at you. Climb something. Stand there and answer for a specific thing you did when you were nineteen. Be mocked, at length, by everyone who raised you, in front of the person you are marrying.

**What it is not.** It is not *save them from us.* Nobody is pretending. The framing is: *your partner is in trouble, and everybody here would like to see exactly how far you will actually go.*

**What it is for.** Two things at once. Every relative gets to discharge every frustration they have ever had about this match without doing the smallest amount of real harm. And everybody gets to watch somebody persevere, visibly, for the person they love, which is the thing the whole institution is supposed to be about and is otherwise almost never demonstrable.

It is done before the wedding rather than as part of it. It is open, it is enjoyed, and it is photographed relentlessly.
