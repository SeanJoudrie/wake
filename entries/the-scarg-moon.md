# THE SCARG MOON
`INTACT`

An entire moon given over to rubbish, and the only place in the galaxy where the past can be dug up.

**Why it exists.** Orbital debris is a problem that compounds, and somebody had to solve it before the lanes became unusable. So the Royals designated a moon, published the designation, and have run it as a public work ever since. It is the single most competent thing that family has done in living memory and almost nobody credits them for it.

**The fungus.** The moon was seeded with an organism that consumes organic matter and nothing else. It is harmless to anything that thinks, it is entirely placid, and it works slowly and completely. Food waste, timber, cloth, bodies of animals, paper: all of it goes, over time, without smoke and without anybody having to manage it.

What is left is everything that does not rot. Metal, plastic, ceramic, glass, machinery, and components.

**Which makes it a mine.** In a galaxy that cannot manufacture, a landfill is not a dump. It is the largest deposit of finished goods anywhere, and people go there to dig.

They are looking for pre-war material: components, tooling, devices, and small nameless objects thrown away eighty years ago by people who had no idea what they were throwing. A drive housing. A working relay. A pair of boots with a matrix in the sole. There is a market for what comes off that moon, there are dealers, and there are certainly forgeries.

**What it is like.** Silent. The fungus has eaten everything that would have rotted, so there is no smell and nothing crawling. Just an enormous, quiet, clean field of objects, going to the horizon, with nobody's name on any of it.

**The record.** The Royal filings on the fungus deployment are precise to the hectare. There is no figure anywhere for how many people are living on that moon.
