# THE SCREAMING MAN
`RECOVERED`

A patient on a Guard ward. No name, no unit, no injury anybody bothered to say out loud.

He is hyperventilating and fighting the hands that are holding him, and the sound he is making is not the sound of pain. It is raw and it comes back off the ceiling, and what is in it is terror and the knowledge that the struggling is not going to work.

They sedate him. He goes quiet. The ward carries on around the space where the noise was.
