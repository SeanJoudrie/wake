# THE SHARED MEAL
`TESTIMONY`

One dish between two people. One bite each is enough.

**What it originally was.** Something difficult. The heart of a beast that had to be hunted first, or an organ nobody would eat by choice, or an egg rare enough that getting one was the labour. The meaning was blunt and everybody understood it: *we will face the unpleasant thing together, and neither of us is going to be the one who does not swallow.*

**What it is now.** Plated. Professionally prepared, seasoned to taste, and in several cases genuinely good. There are chefs who specialise. A few people develop a real fondness for it. Most do not, and most are not expected to.

**The argument at every wedding.** Older relatives think the seasoning is cheating. They say so. They have said so for forty years, and they will say so again this time, and the couple will eat it anyway.

**The variants change the vow.** A heart says *we will face what is dangerous.* An egg says *fertility, and something not yet finished.* Something bitter that must be completed says the hardest thing of the four: *this will not always taste good and you finish it regardless.* And a dish that takes both families three days to prepare says that the labour was the vow and the eating is just the receipt.
