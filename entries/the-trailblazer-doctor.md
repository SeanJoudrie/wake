# THE TRAILBLAZER DOCTOR
`TESTIMONY`

The surgeon at Base Delta. Wears a smug little smirk almost permanently, gives you the bad news fast and flat, and then softens once it has landed.

He is the one who explains floomicide to families: very effective, in very short supply, and it costs you your grip on reality for as long as the healing takes.

**Why he believes in shamans.** He will pull down his collar and show you the scar, a large burned circle in the middle of his chest. A kraskor stampede, while they were clearing ground for a helipad. The horn went through his chest and into his heart.

> *"My mates told me the shaman ran over, murmured strange words, and somehow stopped me from bleeding out. They say he paused my dying body. Animated my legs just long enough to walk back to the car. Then hooked me up to floomicide. Call it magic or whatever you want. But it saved me."*

A man of medicine who takes the local mysticism entirely seriously, because it restarted him.
