# THE VITULA IN THE COMPOUND
`INFERRED`

Not a soldier. A labourer, standing in a place where labourers and soldiers end up looking identical to anyone arriving at speed.

His employer died. He took the next contract, because the next contract had the same terms as the last one and because the terms are the only part of the arrangement a Vitula in that position gets a say in. Nobody asked him what the work was for. Asking is not a step in the process.

He is in the compound for the same reason the floor is in the compound.

The employer's son died there too. Nobody has recorded whether the two of them were in the same room.
