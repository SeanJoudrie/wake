# THE WIPE
*vernacular: the Fry*
`INTACT`

Galaxy-wide electromagnetic event, GC 4401, attributed to C.O.R.D. Destroyed every networked system, every functioning computational device, and every record held in one.

The formal term is used in Guard filings, in law, and in this volume. **The Fry** is the term in general speech and is universal among technicians and serving soldiers. The Restoration notes the vernacular and does not use it.

**Scope.** Total, as far as any surviving evidence indicates. No informant on any world has described a device that continued to function. No archive has reported a surviving data holding. The Restoration has issued a standing request for any contrary evidence and has received none in nineteen years.

**Immediate consequences, documented:**

- Loss of all interstellar communication other than physical courier and unamplified radio
- Loss of all monetary records not held on paper. Currency reverted to paper instruments, then to Guard ledger credits
- Loss of all drone, remote and unmanned systems. Reconstruction is ongoing at Saigo
- Loss of all navigation archives. Slipstream lanes were re-surveyed by hand over approximately thirty years
- Loss of the census

**Paper.** Paper survived. This volume exists because insurers, port authorities, tax offices, monasteries, and shipping clerks kept duplicates in cabinets. The Restoration's holdings are, by weight, approximately eighty per cent commercial and administrative paperwork.

*[Compiler's note: the accounting division of the Guard is understood to have retyped twenty years of financial records by hand following the event. We have applied four times for access to the retyped material and have received three acknowledgements.]*
