# THE WIPE
*vernacular: the Fry*
`INTACT`

In every computer in the galaxy stopped, and everything any of them was holding stopped with it. Paper survived. That is the entire reason this volume exists.

The formal word is the Wipe, and it is what the filings and the law use. Everybody who was actually there calls it the Fry.

**How complete.** Total, as far as anything we can check. In nineteen years nobody on any world has described to us a device that kept working, and no archive anywhere has reported a surviving data holding. We keep the request open.

**What went with it:**

- every network. Communication fell back to couriers, unamplified radio, and towers the churches owned
- every account. Money became paper, then became an entry in a Guard ledger
- every drone and every unmanned system. Saigo is still trying to rebuild them
- every navigational archive. The lanes were re-surveyed by hand over thirty years, and the survey authority publishes what that cost in ships and crews
- the census

**What survived.** Insurers, port authorities, tax offices, monasteries and shipping clerks, all of whom kept duplicates in cabinets because that was the procedure. By weight this archive is about eighty per cent commercial and administrative paperwork.

Which is why we can tell you the tonnage of a cargo that left a world sixty years ago and not how many people were living on it.

*[Compiler's note: the Guard's accounting division is understood to have retyped twenty years of ledger by hand after the event. We have applied four times to see it and have received three acknowledgements.]*
