# TIAM
`TESTIMONY`

A name on the Academy memorial walls. Sixteen at the annual addition in which it appears.

Resmole sector. The garrison casualty returns for that sector are filed by name and average between eleven and nineteen Guard personnel a year.

Attributed to one informant, who gave us the name and the age and asked us to check the wall, and was correct.
