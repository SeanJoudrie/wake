# TORKAL BOMBERS
`INTACT`

The aircraft that burned the jungle colonies, now in its seventh generation and no longer carrying anybody.

**The second generation** is the one people mean when they say the name. Long, heavy, and built to fly low over canopy and unload, and what it unloaded went across the Targath jungle worlds in overlapping lines until there was nothing left standing to overlap. Pilots flew those runs personally, watched an entire ecosystem go up as a sheet of light beneath them, and came home feeling like they had held lightning.

**What changed.** The ionic calibrators in the old batteries were the constant failure point, and nobody fixed them. They were replaced outright with seismic-magnetic cells, which are enormously expensive and last, and the space that bought was used for something else.

**The seventh generation has no pilot.** Real computing has been creeping back into Guard airframes for a few years now, quietly, in ways nobody has to sign for. The men who used to fly these things had a terrible job and knew it. The men who built the thing that replaced them are, in several cases, not comfortable with what they did.

**And afterwards.** They get scrapped. There is a sculpture in a town square on a rock in the Wake field made out of a second-generation airframe, cut up and welded together and painted teal and magenta by somebody who never explained what it was supposed to be. It is the most peaceful thing on that asteroid.
