# TORKAL BOMBERS
`INTACT`

Atmospheric bombing aircraft. Torkal is the manufacturer and not a people.

**Generations.** Two. The first is pre-war. The second entered service after it and is in current use, being one of the few airframes in continuous production, the type being simple enough to build with surviving tooling.

**Employment.** Area bombardment of surface targets. Sortie returns are filed to the standard of all Guard air operations: aircraft, crew, sortie time, ordnance loaded, ordnance released, grid struck.

**Recorded employment against jungle settlement, Targath range:** filed in full. Sorties, tonnage, and grids struck are all recorded. Population of the grids struck is not recorded, no census of those settlements having been taken by any party.
