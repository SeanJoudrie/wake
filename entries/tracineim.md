# TRACINEIM
`TESTIMONY`

Faith of the divine feminine. State religion of the Royal systems.

**Doctrine, as reported.** Tracine is the generative principle. Trees, worlds, water and anything that brings life are read as feminine, and the universe entire is held to be so. The faith is not primarily concerned with conduct and does not maintain a code.

**History.** Attested first among the powerless. Early external references, where they survive, are dismissive, and characterise it as a faith of slaves, women and children. It was subsequently adopted by warrior societies, became respectable, and became establishment. The Restoration notes that this progression is not unusual and can be traced in three other faiths in this volume.

**Practice.** Embedded rather than professed. There is no congregational obligation, no weekly assembly and no clergy of the kind other faiths maintain. The characteristic act is a blessing spoken in parting, rendered in Galactic Standard as *May Traceen be with you*, used across the Royal systems by observant and unobservant speakers alike and frequently by people who would not describe themselves as believers.

**The crown.** The reigning queen is identified with Tracine and is not held to be immortal. The crown has never claimed that she cannot die. It claims that she is the presence of Tracine on Shayron, which is a tenure and not a lifespan.

**Documentation.** Almost none. The faith was carried in people and in speech. The Restoration holds no scripture, no canon, and no register of adherents, and notes that the state religion of the wealthiest polity in the galaxy is filed in this volume at the same tier as a story told to us in a kitchen.
