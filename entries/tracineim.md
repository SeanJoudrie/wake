# TRACINEIM
`TESTIMONY`

The faith of the divine feminine, and the state religion of the Royal systems.

Tracine is the generative principle. Trees are feminine. Worlds are feminine. Water, growth, anything that brings life. The universe itself is read as feminine, and the faith is not much interested in telling you how to behave. It has no code and no list of sins.

**It started at the bottom.** The oldest outside references to it are contemptuous: a religion for slaves, women and children. Then warrior cultures picked it up, and then it became respectable, and then it became the establishment, which is a road several faiths in this volume have walked.

**How it is actually practised.** Barely, and constantly. There is no weekly assembly, no obligation, and no clergy in the way other faiths have clergy. What there is, is a blessing people say when they part:

> *May Traceen be with you.*

Said by the devout, said by people who have not thought about it in years, and said by soldiers who would tell you flatly they believe none of it.

**The Queen.** She is not held to be immortal. The crown never claimed she could not die. It claimed she is Tracine's presence on Shayron, which is not a lifespan. It is a tenure.
