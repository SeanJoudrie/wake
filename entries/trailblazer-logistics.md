# TRAILBLAZER LOGISTICS
`INTACT`

Operating arm of the Trailblazers. Sectioned by function.

Sections are numbered and are filed with the concession authority of each world in which they operate. Recorded sections include survey and seismic elevation, port construction, freight handling, medical, and salvage recovery.

Section personnel work on contract to the section and not to the world, which places them outside local labour registers. The arrangement is noted in three concession instruments and objected to in none.
