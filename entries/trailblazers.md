# THE TRAILBLAZERS
`INTACT`

Soldiers from both sides of a war nobody won, building ports for worlds the shipping lanes forgot. Grey coveralls, orange trim, and a shovel on the badge.

**Who they are.** Veterans of the Autumnal party and of C.O.R.D., in proportions the body does not record because its founding instrument states that prior service is not a matter for enquiry. As far as we can tell that is actually observed.

**What they do.** The war cut the lanes and whole sectors went dark. The Guard holds a monopoly on the little that still moves. The Trailblazers reopen the routes, build the port where none survived, and run it under concession: fixed term, revenue split with the local council, paid for in land and housing.

They are the only organised logistics network in the settled galaxy operating outside Guard control, and they got there by dealing with local councils rather than with the Guard at all.

**What it cost them.** The founding accounts are held. The body ran at a loss for eleven years and its people went unpaid for seven of them. The arrears schedule is filed. It was settled in full.

**No walls.** Every Trailblazer installation in the concession filings has no perimeter, no watchtower, no emplacement and no armed establishment. We checked this against the construction schedules, which list no such item, and against three informants, who confirmed it with some amusement.

**Standing.** They hold seats on local councils on at least four worlds, in every case by the council's own instrument and not by any treaty.
