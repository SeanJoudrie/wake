# THE TRAILBLAZERS
`INTACT`

Civilian trade and construction body. Builds and operates ports on isolated worlds under revenue-sharing concession with local authorities. Founded and staffed principally by veterans of both belligerent parties.

**Composition.** Former soldiers of the Autumnal party and of C.O.R.D., in numbers the body itself does not distinguish and does not record. Its own founding instrument states that prior service is not a matter for enquiry. This appears to be observed.

**Business.** Reopening of trade routes cut by the war, construction of port facilities where none survived, and operation of those facilities under concession. The standard concession runs to a fixed term, splits revenue with the local council, and is paid for in land and housing.

**Standing.** The only organised body operating a logistics network outside Guard control. The Guard holds a monopoly on movement in most of the settled galaxy. The Trailblazers are the exception, and their concessions are with local authorities rather than with the Guard.

**Finances.** The founding accounts are held. The body operated at a loss for its first eleven years and its personnel were unpaid for seven of them. The wage arrears schedule is filed. It was settled in full in GC 4443.

**Establishment.** Grey working dress with orange facing. The device is a shovel. Bases carry no defensive works of any kind, a fact catalogued under Base Delta and true of every Trailblazer installation in the concession filings.

**Local standing.** Trailblazer representatives hold seats on local councils on at least four worlds, in each case by the council's own instrument and not by treaty.
