# TSPA
`INTACT`

Worked moon of the Polaris system. Soy and pulse protein.

**System:** Polaris
**Status:** local government retained. Licensing held on Kelton
**Population:** between 3 million and 20 million
**Membership:** application and assessment
**Calendars in use:** Polaris Harvest Reckoning
**Principal exports:** protein paste, protein cake, pulse flour

Wet, warm, and terraced across the whole worked belt. Filed output GC 4461: 88,140,900 tonnes.

Tspa ships bulk protein and not luxury food. Its product appears in the ration schedules of eleven member worlds and on the menu of none of them.
