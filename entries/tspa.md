# TSPA
`INTACT`

A warm, wet moon terraced from pole to pole, and the reason a Guardsman's ration pack tastes the way it does.

Every workable surface is stepped into paddies. Soy and pulse, grown wet, harvested wet, and processed within a day into paste, cake and flour. Eighty-eight million tonnes a year, none of it interesting.

**The Tspa fact.** Its protein appears in the ration schedules of eleven member worlds and on the menus of none of them. It is what feeds soldiers, prisoners, colonists, and anybody else whose food is a line item rather than a choice.

Which means most of the galaxy has eaten a great deal of Tspa without ever hearing the name. Say it to a Guardsman and he will not recognise it. Hand him a protein cake and he will recognise it instantly, and pull a face.
