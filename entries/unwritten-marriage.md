# UNWRITTEN MARRIAGE
`TESTIMONY`

Two versions of the same idea, and the people who practise them find each other completely reasonable and everybody else exhausting.

**The first has no word for it.** Lifelong partnerships form, hold, raise children and end in one death rather than two, and nothing is signed, worn, announced or celebrated. Asked when they married, people from these worlds are not being evasive. The question does not parse. They were not married. They are together, and have been.

**The second is purely administrative.** Live together long enough and you are married. No ceremony, no announcement, no date anybody could name. The threshold is a duration and the duration is written down somewhere in a municipal code that nobody has read.

These are not cold people. They have every feeling anybody else has. They simply do not perform them in public, and they regard a world that does as a world in which everybody is auditioning. Other cultures find them boring. They find other cultures tiring. Neither has ever been persuaded.

**The interruption.** Because the second version runs on a clock, it has a loophole, and the loophole has survived every attempt to close it: sleep elsewhere a stated number of nights each year and the count resets.

Which means somewhere there is a person who has spent forty years going to their sister's house for three nights every year, on the same dates, for reasons the family stopped asking about a long time ago.

**And the open problem.** A species that already regards itself as one enormous family has a genuinely strange time with all of this, and the Restoration has not been able to get a straight answer from anybody about how it works.
