# VANCE
`RECOVERED`

A man who will argue with you about what is true, and will not raise his voice while he does it.

He has a position and it is not a complicated one: that being right is a smaller thing than being honest, and that most people who are pleased with how clever they are have quietly stopped doing the harder of the two. He puts it plainly, without ornament, and he puts it the same way the fourth time as the first.

**Why it lands badly.** Because he is unbothered. The person opposite him gets angrier as it goes and he does not move, and there is nothing more infuriating than a man who is not competing. Called weak for it, he takes that as a description rather than an insult and continues.
