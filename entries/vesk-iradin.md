# COLONEL VESK IRADIN
`CONTESTED`

A colonel of the Guard's Strategic Covert Operations Division, and a signature.

That is the whole of him. There is a rank, a division, and a name, and the name appears where an authorising officer's name appears, in the place on a form where somebody has to be accountable and where the person who is accountable is by design a person nobody has met.

No posting history. No unit. No photograph, no service dates, nothing filed anywhere that would let you work out whether he was ever a lieutenant.

A division that operates covertly does not publish its colonels, which is either the reason this record is empty or the excuse for it.

**[SPOILER] What he signed.** Operation Thorn Crown. The name in the authorising line belongs to the plan to end Shayron's independence by staging an attack on the anniversary of the Queen's Ascension and installing a monarch of the Guard's choosing.

The record is empty in exactly the way the operation requires it to be.
