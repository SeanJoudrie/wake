# VICK
`RECOVERED`

A propagandist. He made the Guard look good for a living, and then he was standing outside a hangar on Sonaff with a rifle in his hands.

His hands shook while he was fumbling with it. He knew it was not going to help.

When the raiders came out of the storm, he was the one who charged them.
