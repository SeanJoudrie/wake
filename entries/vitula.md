# THE VITULA
`CONTESTED`

Two metres and more. Grey, and wet to the touch. Eight white eyes set around the head, so that there is no direction they cannot see. On the back, folded and useless, a pair of wings that have not carried anything in a very long time.

**The body.** Insect-descended, and without the shell you would expect from that: all of the mass is muscle and nerve. They breathe badly off their own worlds and wear masks everywhere else, bought by the employer and deducted from the wage.

Pain registers in them below the human mean. That is a property of the nervous system, documented in the pre-war literature, and it means what it says: they feel it less, so they keep working.

**The disagreement.** Guard instructional material says the Vitula have a collective mind and no individual will. The surviving biology says pheromone signalling, proximity awareness, and a distress sense good to about 1.6 kilometres that carries no content beyond the fact that somebody is hurt.

Both are printed here. They describe different animals.

**Work.** Excavation, tunnelling, deep foundation, haulage, foundry, and armed contract. Vitula crews appear in the construction record of eleven worlds, including as subcontractors on the Kitalia pylons, listed by crew and by rate. The kingdoms themselves are small, farmed at the surface and mined underneath.

When a king or queen dies, the pattern in the record is not succession crisis and not reprisal. It is a cluster of new engagements. The crews go and find whoever is hiring. This has been observed four times.

**One further fact.** Guard field instruction includes anatomy for this people, specifying the location of the heart. The manual is `INTACT`, is in current issue, and places that lesson at the same point in the syllabus as the lesson on how to operate a weapon.
