# THE VITULA
`CONTESTED`

Non-human people of several minor kingdoms and extensive tunnel systems. Insect-descended. Present across the labour markets of the outer worlds.

**Physical.** Approximately two metres. Grey, moist integument. Eight eyes distributed about the head, affording no blind aspect. Vestigial wings, non-functional, retained. No exoskeleton, the mass being muscle and nerve. Atmospheric masks are worn off their own worlds as a matter of course.

Pain response is measurably below the human mean. This is a property of the nervous system and is documented in the pre-war medical literature.

**The contested point.** Guard instructional material describes the Vitula as possessing a collective mind and as acting without individual volition. The surviving pre-war biological literature describes pheromone signalling, proximity awareness, and a weak distress perception effective to approximately 1.6 kilometres and carrying no content beyond the fact of distress. The two accounts are printed together. They are not compatible.

**Economy.** Excavation, tunnelling, deep construction, heavy haulage, foundry work, and armed contract labour. Vitula crews appear in the construction records of eleven worlds, including as sub-contractors on the Kitalia pylon programme, where they are listed by crew and by rate. Kingdoms are small, agricultural at the surface, and mining below it.

On the death of a king or queen, the recorded pattern is not succession crisis and not reprisal. The crews seek the next contract. This appears in the contracting record as a cluster of new engagements following each recorded royal death, and has been observed four times.

**Note.** Guard field instruction includes anatomical instruction on this people, specifying the location of the heart. The relevant manual is `INTACT`, is in current issue, and the instruction appears at the same point in the syllabus as instruction on the operation of a weapon.
