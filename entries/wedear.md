# WEDEAR
`INTACT`

Officer school, and the place a career either turns or does not.

A cold, orderly world of lecture halls, map rooms and parade squares, where roughly four thousand people a year arrive convinced they are about to be given something.

Around two thousand nine hundred of them leave with a commission.

**The rest go back.** Returned to prior rank, in the language of the schedule, which means you rejoin your old unit at your old grade in front of everybody who watched you leave. The proportion sent back has never once dropped below a quarter, in any year on record, and Wedear does not appear to regard this as a problem to be solved.

Nobody talks about it afterward. A man who failed Wedear will mention Actom, mention his MOS, mention every posting since, and simply omit the year in between. It comes up eventually, usually late, usually once.
