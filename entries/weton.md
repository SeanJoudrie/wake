# WETON
`INTACT`

Principal growing planet of the Polaris system. Grain at planetary scale.

**System:** Polaris
**Status:** local government retained. Licensing held on Kelton
**Population:** between 60 million and 300 million
**Membership:** application and assessment. Settlement is granted against a holding
**Calendars in use:** Polaris Harvest Reckoning
**Principal exports:** raw grain

**Production.** Filed harvest, GC 4461: 1,204,880,600 tonnes raw grain, of which 1,204,880,600 tonnes were purchased at the scheduled price. The system purchase guarantee has been met in every year on record.

**Dependency.** Weton's soil is maintained by fertiliser rendered on Sheel. Its water is allocated from Corr. Its seed lines are licensed from Tall. Its grain leaves the world raw and is milled on Dorrow. Weton holds none of these functions and has never sought to.

**Character.** Almost no accumulated wealth. Wages are good and are paid in scrip. Holdings are large, mechanised, and worked by families under long-term settlement instruments. The machinery is pre-war, irreplaceable, and maintained under the system repair entitlement by yards whose senior mechanics are the most consequential people in any given district.
