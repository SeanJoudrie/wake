# WETON
`INTACT`

Grain from one horizon to the other, worked by families on long instruments with machinery nobody can build any more.

**System:** Polaris
**Status:** local government retained. Licensing held on Kelton
**Population:** between 60 million and 300 million
**Membership:** application and assessment. Settlement is granted against a holding
**Calendars in use:** Polaris Harvest Reckoning
**Principal exports:** raw grain

**Harvest, GC 4461:** 1,204,880,600 tonnes, of which 1,204,880,600 tonnes were bought at the scheduled price. The purchase guarantee has been met in every year on record.

**What Weton does not have.** Its soil comes from Sheel. Its water is allocated from Corr. Its seed is licensed from Tall. Its grain leaves raw and is milled on Dorrow. It holds none of those functions and has never asked for one.

**The machines.** Every combine and hauler here is pre-war and irreplaceable, kept running under the system repair entitlement by yards whose senior mechanics are the most consequential people in any given district. A farmer here can afford to fix the tractor. That is not true anywhere else in this archive and everybody here knows it.

Wages are good. Wages are in scrip.
