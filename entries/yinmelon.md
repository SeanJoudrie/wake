# YINMELON
`INTACT`

A big, heavy, fleshy fruit, mostly processed into a sauce of the same name and shipped in sealed tins by the container load.

It is one of the highest-volume food items moving anywhere, which is exactly why it is also one of the most common things written on the outside of a container holding something else entirely. Customs seizure returns are full of Yinmelon sauce that is not Yinmelon sauce.

The sauce itself is decent. Somebody has a story about a guard at a farmer's market on Monver who took a bribe in the form of a taste and lost a thumb to what was in the tin.
