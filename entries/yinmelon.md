# YINMELON
`INTACT`

Large fleshy fruit, cultivated widely, principally processed into a sauce of the same name.

The sauce is shipped in sealed tins in bulk and is one of the highest-volume food items in the freight returns. The seizure returns record it as one of the most frequent declared contents of containers found to hold something else.
